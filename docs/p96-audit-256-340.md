# Pass 96 comprehensive audit, lane p96-d: movements 256–340

- **Reviewer:** Claude Opus 5.5, lane p96-d, 2026-09-28. Five forked sub-lanes covered 257–273 (d1), 274–290 (d2), 291–307 (d3), 308–324 (d4) and 325–340 (d5); 256 was checked centrally. Their findings are merged here.
- **Mode:** read-only, on the working tree at commit 7ce1e83. Nothing under `src/`, `tests/` or `docs/` was edited except this report.
- **Captures:** every movement has two sheets in `/dev/shm/p96/d/cap/NNN/`:
  - `tile.png`: the plate and eleven views. These are the default view (v0), yaw +50°/pitch 20° (yp50), yaw −50°/pitch −15° (ym50), left, right, back, top (pitch 70°), below (pitch −60°), phases 0.33 and 0.66, and phase 0.33 rotated (p33r).
  - `strip.png`: 12 phases over the whole cycle, in the default view.

  Aimed zooms are in `/dev/shm/p96/d/dN/` (d5's are in `d5/z/`). The tools are `/dev/shm/p96/d/cap.py` and `/dev/shm/p96/d/shots.mjs`, which ran against a private vite server on port 47496. Relative capture paths below are relative to `/dev/shm/p96/d/`.
- **Screens:** disconnected-parts, coincident-faces, white-parts, edge-mounts and faceting were run over 256–340. The raw output is in `/dev/shm/p96/d/scr-*.json`.
  - A lead is reported only where a zoom or a measurement confirmed it.
  - **White parts:** no hits.
  - **Edge mounts:** the only real hit is 332; the others are hidden.
  - **Visible flicker:** 285 and 318 only. 265's lead is internal to the bore.
- **Motion:** every movement was checked over the 12-phase strip. d4 and d5 also ran bounding-box probes over two periods.
  - The only loop jumps in the range are the intended escapement drops (309–313) and 321's winding stroke.
  - Rotation direction was measured against the plate's arrow for 291, 293, 296–298, 302, 303 and 305, and all match.
- **Ledger:** each movement's `visibleFlaws` and `limits`, and the p93–p95 review mentions, were read first. Documented forced flaws are re-reported only where a concrete fix exists, and marked "open since pN".
