# Pass 104 audit, movements 1–85 (fifth comprehensive pass)

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, audit lane r001. The lane was split into four forked sub-lanes: A (1–21), B (22–43), C (44–64) and D (65–85). The lead confirmed every medium finding on its capture. Date: 2026-09-29. This was a read-only audit of the working tree at `ad638ba`. Nothing under `src/`, `tests/` or `docs/` was edited apart from this file, and no git writes were made.

## Method

- **Server and captures:** a vite server on port 46081, with Playwright (`chromium.launch({channel:'chrome', headless:true})`) driving `/dev/shm/p104/r001/shots.mjs`, a copy of the p90 helper.
- **Tiles:** one per movement at `/dev/shm/p104/r001/NNN/tile.png` (produced by `/dev/shm/p104/r001/std.sh`). Each shows the plate, the default view, phases 0.33 and 0.66, left (yaw −50°, pitch 15°), right (+50°), top (pitch 80°), behind (yaw 180°) and below (pitch −60°). The single views are alongside, in `NNN/*.png`.
- **Strips and zooms:** ten-phase strips (0, 0.02, 0.125 … 0.875, 0.98) were taken where motion matters, and close zooms were taken of joints, eyes, teeth, hooks, pawls and ropes. They are in `/dev/shm/p104/r001/z{A,B,C,D}/`.
- **Screens:** all five ran over 1–85, with output in `/dev/shm/p104/r001/`:
  - `disconnected-parts.json`
  - `coincident-faces.json`
  - `white-parts.json`
  - `edge-mounts.json`
  - `faceting.json`
- **Ledger and earlier reviews:** every ledger row for 1–85 was read, along with docs/p101-audit-001-085.md and the p101–p103 fix reviews for this range.

## Findings, 1–21 (sub-lane A)

### 013, 014, 015, 016, 017, 018: low (p101 finding still open)
- **Flaw:** the free tip of each load (lower) hook is still an uncapped tube, and at 25× you can see into the tube. The screen's `openEnds` are 13 (0.69, −1.95), 14 (0.26, −1.93), 15 (0.13, −2.99), 16 and 17 (−0.51, −1.78), and 18 (0.065, −2.38). 019 already has a `hook-tip-cap`.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/mtips.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/mtipZ.png` (013 and 016 at 25×, with 019's capped tip for comparison).
- **Fix:** add 019's hemispherical `hook-tip-cap` (tube radius, on the tube's end frame) to the shared hook builder used by 12–18.

### 012–021 (pulley stirrups): low
- **Flaw:** the sheave stirrups are stacked box bars. The top crossbar is thicker than, and offset from, the side straps, so every top corner shows a step or lip. The hook shank sits on a separate stacked collar block. This shows in every rotated view.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/m012b.png` (012's corner at 14×), `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/m012.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/mtips.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/mtipU.png` (015's collar).
- **Fix:** make each stirrup one constant-section strap swept along a U path (or one extruded U profile) with small bend radii. The hook shank should rise from a round boss on the crown. Share the builder across 12–21.

### 012: low
- **Flaw:** the weight bag is a low-poly lathe (the faceting screen gives a maximum angle of 24.8° over 128 edges). Its hard creases at the shoulder and belly make it read as a turned vase rather than a soft sack.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/m012b.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/m012.png`.
- **Fix:** regenerate the profile as a smooth spline with at least 48 points (rounded shoulder, round bottom), keeping the tied neck and eye.

### 014: low
- **Flaw:** the five sheave cheeks (Ø1.08) stand about 0.065 per side proud of the crown skin (0.95 wide), so the top and oblique views show rows of small nubs along both edges of each block.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/m014.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/014/top.png`.
- **Fix:** give the crown skin the cheeks' radius (0.54), or trim the cheeks flush with the skin.

### 007: low
- **Flaw:** bevel A (ochre) and bevel C (tan) are nearly the same yellow, so where they mesh at the upper left of the default view they merge into one yellow mass.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/007/tile.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zA/m007.png`.
- **Fix:** recolour C to steel grey or brick.

Clean: 001, 002, 003, 004, 005, 006, 008, 009, 010, 011. 019, 020 and 021 carry only the shared stirrup low.

## Findings, 22–43 (sub-lane B)

### 038: low (p101 finding, deferred by p101-f1, still open)
- **Flaw:** where each stepped sector's straight flank meets its tip teeth, there is a stray step or notch, and the last tip tooth is a narrow half-tooth.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zB/m38.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zB/strip038.png`.
- **Fix:** give `steppedSectorCut` an outline pass: straight radial flanks tangent to a tip arc concentric with the gear, whole teeth spaced symmetrically, and a partial end tooth trimmed to a rounded land.

### 027: low
- **Flaw 1:** Brown's domed hub with a small knob at the drum's centre is missing; the six webs just meet in a dark point.
- **Flaw 2:** the drum's back disc uses the dark pocket-floor colour (#1d3b4b), so from behind the whole drum reads as a black disc.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/027/tile.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zB/m27.png`.
- **Fix:** add a low domed boss of about 0.2 R at the web junction, clear of the carrier plate (z < 0.36). Colour only the recessed pocket floor dark, and give the outer back face the body colour (#315f78).

### 032: low
- **Flaw:** Brown draws a double outer circle on both friction wheels, but the large blue wheel is a plain disc.
- **Capture:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/032/tile.png`.
- **Fix:** add a shallow rim band or chamfer ring at about 0.95 R on its faces, as on 024 and 030.

### 023: low
- **Flaw:** take-up pulley A's stirrup is lopsided. Its rear arm stands 0.13 behind the sheave, but its front arm stands 0.27 in front (z +0.5, pushed out to meet the rope-B plane), so it reads as a wide, off-centre frame.

## Findings, 44–64 (sub-lane C)

### 050, 051: medium (shared universal-joint builder)
- **Flaw 1:** every fork eye, and every eye of 50's figure-8 frame, is a round boss stacked on a square-ended arm. A step shows where they meet, and the arm's square corners stick out beside the boss.
- **Flaw 2:** the black cross arms end as square boxes butting the eyes, and in oblique views these show past the boss rim.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m50z.png` (12×; see panels 50z-b, 51z-a and 51z-c), `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m5051.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m51y.png`.
- **Fix:** extrude each fork (and 50's frame) as one outline whose ends are arcs concentric with the pin, full depth, with tangent or filleted joins. End each cross arm in a round trunnion.

### 048: medium (p101 low, still open and worse than described)
- **Flaw:** the bell crank's horizontal arm ends in a three-quarter disc that is not concentric with the rod pin. The pin sits in the lower left of the disc, and the disc is cut by a vertical flat on its left and a horizontal notch at its bottom. It is clear from the front and from behind.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m48b.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m48.png`.
- **Fix:** make the arm one extrusion ending in a full arc concentric with the pin (outer radius about 1.6× the pin radius), tangent to the arm edges, like the clean elbow (`zC/48-elbow.png`). Keep the rod's rounded top concentric with the same pin.

### 054: medium (p101 low, still open)
- **Flaw:** block A's linings are separate stepped half-discs with lips inside the cut-outs, so it reads as a pile of pieces. The faceting score is 200.7, with smoothed creases up to 58.9° and jagged clusters.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m54.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/054/tile.png`.
- **Fix:** extrude A and its linings as one profile, with a smooth arc for each seat and no stepped lips. Keep the collar channel.

### 046: low (p101 low, still open)
- **Flaw:** both chain ends are unanchored. The fusee end is a bare cut end lying on the bottom tread. The barrel end starts abruptly partway round the barrel wall, with its end link standing off the drum.
- **Capture:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m46.png`.
- **Fix:** hook the fusee end onto a pin or slot in the base flange where the ledge runs out, and hook the barrel end onto a stud on the barrel wall.

### 053: low
- **Flaw 1:** a flat rectangular brass bar lies across the double clutch's neck and groove, behind the lever pin. Brown does not draw it, and it reads as a stray stick.
- **Flaw 2:** the jaws do not match. The hubs' jaws are flat-topped trapezoids and the clutch's are sharp triangles, so dark gaps remain when they are engaged.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m53k.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m53.png`.
- **Fix:** replace the bar with an arc shoe (or two pins) in the collar groove, and cut both jaw sets from one zigzag profile so they seat fully.

### 058: low
- **Flaw:** the brown belt nearly vanishes against the copper-brown first lower pulley at phase 0.33.
- **Capture:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zC/m5861.png`.
- **Fix:** recolour that pulley grey or blue.

## Findings, 65–85 (sub-lane D)

### 081: medium

Brown draws a plain solid rack-rod, and the motion is scripted, so the pin does nothing.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zD/z081d.png` (behind, 7×), `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zD/z081e.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zD/z081c.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/081/tile.png`.

### 082: low

- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zD/m82.png`, `/dev/shm/p104/r001/zD/z082b.png`.

### 077: low
