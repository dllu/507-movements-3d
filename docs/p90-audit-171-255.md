## Screens

- **Disconnected parts** (`disc.json`, `disc.log`, summary in `disc-summary.txt`): each DET, SLIVER, LIPS and OPENENDS lead was checked in the captures. Most "floating" entries are fixed pivots with no drawn frame (233, 235, 240, 241, 245), as expected. Only 251's undrawn pile and anvil are reported as a flaw. 171's link-pin joint and 176's wrist-pin boss window match the visual findings above; the other near-misses are not visible at render scale.
- **Coincident faces** (`cf.json`, `cf.log`): 11 pairs pass the filter (contrast ≥ 0.1, area ≥ 1e-4): 196, 197, 202, 208, 233 ×2, 241, 248 ×3 and 254. Every one is already in p89's latent list (0 of 10 views visible), so none is new. Aimed zooms on the strongest pairs below the filter (188 leaf spring/lug at 8.5e-5; 207 wheel/hub; 176 selector; 212 stop) show no flicker (`cf/tile.png`). Faint diagonal shadow-acne striping shows on some large faces at high zoom (191, 207, 176); this is low severity, fixed by a slightly larger shadow normalBias.
