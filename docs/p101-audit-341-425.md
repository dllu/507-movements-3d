- **Reviewer:** Claude Opus 5.5, pass-101 audit lane for 341–425 (single lane, no sub-forks).
- **Date:** 2026-09-28, at HEAD 253e748 plus uncommitted e2e-spec edits (no src changes in the tree).
- **Scope:** read-only. This report is the only file written. No source, test, ledger or git changes.

## Method

- **Captures:** a private vite server on port 46025 (stopped afterwards with `fuser -k`). The capture script is `/dev/shm/p101/e/shots.mjs`, the p90 helper with longer timeouts.
  - Each movement has twelve views in `/dev/shm/p101/e/cap/<id>-<v>.png`:
    - `d`: default.
    - `L` and `R`: yaw ∓50°, pitch 15°.
    - `T`: pitch 65°.
    - `B`: behind.
    - `U`: below, pitch −50°.
    - `p1`–`p4`: phases 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8.
    - `Lp`: yaw −35° at phase 0.5.
    - `X`: yaw 30°, pitch 40° at phase 0.3. This is the tilt view for flat or top-down defaults.
  - `/dev/shm/p101/e/tiles/<id>.png` puts the plate beside all twelve views. All 85 tiles were inspected against the plate.
  - Motion strips (10 phases, including 0.02 and 0.98 for the loop seam) were made for 351, 353, 360, 364, 370, 371, 389, 391, 398, 402, 412 and 415, in `strip/m<id>.png`. For 370, phases 1.0 and 2.0 confirm that the mirror's indexing accumulates across loops (`z/m370.png`).
  - Zooms are in `/dev/shm/p101/e/z/`, and numeric bounding boxes came from `bbox.mjs`.
  - **All capture paths below are relative to `/dev/shm/p101/e/`.**
- **Screens:** all five screens ran over 341–425 into `scr/`: `disc.json`, `cf.json`, `white.json`, `edge.json` and `facet.json`. There is a per-ID digest in `scr/disc-summary.txt`. Leads were verified visually or numerically, or dismissed (see the end).
- **Ledger and history:** each row's `visibleFlaws` and `limits` was read, along with p96-audit-341-425 and the p97–p100 reviews touching this range.
  - p96 lows that are still open are re-reported briefly, marked "(p96 repeat)".
  - Documented forced flaws are not re-reported unless a fix is concrete.
