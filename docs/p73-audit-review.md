# Pass 73 audits of the rows changed in passes 67–72

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5 (read-only audit lanes p73-audit-a and p73-audit-b), 2026-09-26/27.

Scope: the 101 movements changed in passes 67–72 and rated reasonable. Each was compared with its plate in the default view and inspected rotated ±60°, from the back and top, at several phases including near the loop seam, zoomed on working contacts, water and engine passages. Loop seams, intersections (engines at a coarser spacing where the standard screen ran out of memory) and faces were screened.
