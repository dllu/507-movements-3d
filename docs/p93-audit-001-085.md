# Pass 93 audit, movements 1–85 (lane p93-a)

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, lane p93-a. The lane audited 1–21 and 44–85 itself; forked sub-lane p93-a2 audited 22–43, and its findings are merged below. Date: 2026-09-28. This was a read-only audit of the working tree at `10a65f3`. Nothing under `src/`, `tests/` or `docs/` was edited apart from this file, and no git writes were made.

## Method

- **Server:** a vite server on port 45931 served the working tree. Captures were made with Playwright (`chromium.launch({channel:'chrome', headless:true})`) using `/dev/shm/p93/a/shots.mjs`, which adds `pick` (raycast from a default-view pixel), `abs` (a world target) and `focus` targets. p93-a2 used `/dev/shm/p93/a/shots2.mjs`, which adds role and index targets.
- **Contact sheets:** one per movement, at `/dev/shm/p93/a/sheets/<id>.png`. Each has eight tiles: the plate, the default view at phase 0, right (yaw +50°, pitch 20°), left (yaw −50°, pitch −15°), top (pitch 75°), behind (yaw 180°), and phases 0.33 and 0.66. The single shots are in `/dev/shm/p93/a/sh/` and the zooms are in `/dev/shm/p93/a/z/`.
- **Screens:** all ran over 1–85, with output in `/dev/shm/p93/a/`:
  - `disc.json`: `screen-disconnected-parts.mjs`
  - `cf.json`: `screen-coincident-faces.mjs`
  - `white.json`: `screen-white-parts.mjs`
  - `edge.json`: `screen-edge-mounts.mjs`
  - `facet.json`: `screen-faceting.mjs`
- **Ledger:** every row in `docs/movement-status.json` for 1–85 was read, along with the p90-fa, p91 and p92 reviews that touch the range. Documented forced flaws are only repeated where a fix looks available.
- **Checks on every sheet:**
  - tooth, lobe and pin counts;
  - rack, worm and ratchet handedness;
  - rotation direction against the plate's arrows (by comparing phases 0, 0.33 and 0.66);
  - rope colour and tautness, white parts, shadows, and pins on edges;
  - see-through treatment, and undrawn extras.

## Findings, 22–43 (sub-lane p93-a2)

### 031 — medium
- The worm's helix is the wrong hand. On the plate the front flanks of the three threads lean "/" (running down to the left), but on the model they lean "\" (down to the right), so the worm is mirrored. I re-checked this against the sibling worms: 64, 66 and 67 all lean "\" on both the plate and the model (/dev/shm/p93/a/z/worms.png, /dev/shm/p93/a/z/worms2.png). Only 31 is reversed.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p93/a/z/31-worm.png (the plate crop above, the default view below), /dev/shm/p93/a/sheets/31.png.
- Fix: flip the sign of the worm's lead in the 31 worm builder. Then reverse the worm's spin or the wheel's rotation sign so the mesh stays conjugate, and rerun the 31 model test and the mesh screen.

### 037 — low
- Each yellow stud head has a pinched, star-shaped shading artefact at its pole (the faceting screen's jagged-surface hit on four meshes of 4096 triangles). At normal zoom it reads as a small "+" on every stud. Each head also has a small flat notch at one side of its rim.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p93/a/z/37-stud2.png, /dev/shm/p93/a/z/zf.png (lower left).
- Fix: build the heads as lathed caps whose pole normals are averaged (or with a tiny flat at the pole), and clip the relief cut so it doesn't flatten the rim. The documented residuals (two heads break the silhouette; 17 flutes against about 28) are unchanged.

### 038 — low
- The grey frame link between the hubs is a thin round rod, about 1/12 of the washer diameter. Its ends butt against the outer rims of the hub washers, where they should form eyes. The plate draws a flat bar running into both hub rings.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p93/a/z/zl.png (top row), /dev/shm/p93/a/z/38-d.png.
- Fix: replace the rod with one flat link extrusion in the washer plane. Give it an eye concentric with each shaft (outer radius equal to the washer radius) and a waist about 0.4 of the hub-ring diameter, and merge the washers into the eyes.

### 029 — low
- The spiral is a black, round-section wire lying on the orange disc. The plate draws a flat-topped rib in the disc's own material. The ledger calls the round section an inference, but nothing forces it.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p93/a/sheets/29.png.
- Fix: extrude a rectangular-section rib (width about one tooth space, height about one addendum) in the disc's colour and in one piece with the disc, on the same centreline.

### 043 — low

### 057 — medium

The plate's ring is also finer-pitched: it has visibly more teeth than the model's 34, a count the ledger lists as a residual.

### 046 — medium

- Capture: /dev/shm/p93/a/z/fm.png (bottom right), /dev/shm/p93/a/sheets/46.png.

### 063 — medium

- The spring at the left is a rectangular wire hairpin: two parallel rods joined at the end, which show as a loop in the right and top views. The plate draws one straight, flat spring strip.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p93/a/sheets/63.png (right and top tiles), /dev/shm/p93/a/z/63m.png.
