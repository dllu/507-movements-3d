## Findings (ordered by ID)

### 87 (low; open since p93)
- **Flaw 1:** the gold reversing stud on wheel E still straddles the band's inner edge (edge ratio 0.65).
- **Flaw 2:** the rear input shaft's end face (z −1.05) is coplanar with both hidden spokes' back faces (flicker screen, contrast 0.52).
- **Capture:** `tiles/087.png`, `edge.json`, `cf.json`.
- **Fix:**
  - Move the stud to the band's mid-radius, or shrink its boss to about 0.8× the band width.
  - End the shaft 0.005 inside the hub (z −1.055).

### 90, 91 (low; open since p90)
- **Flaw:** the yoke stems are still round rods; Brown draws flat bars filleted into the yoke. 91's frame outline is still slightly skewed.
- **Capture:** `tiles/090.png`, `tiles/091.png`.
- **Fix:** make the stems a rectangular section in the yoke extrusion, with fillets. Rebuild 91's outline from level, mirror-symmetric lines and arcs.

### 99 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the guide rails are still thin wire posts that end free at both ends. The grey block reaches them only by a bracket.
- **Capture:** `tiles/099.png`.
- **Fix:** thicken the rails to about 0.16, and make the block and its brackets one extrusion that reaches them.

### 102, 103 (low; open since p90)
- **Flaw:** the square threads are still deep thin fins with narrow lands. Brown's square threads have land ≈ groove.
- **Capture:** `tiles/102.png`, `tiles/103.png`.
- **Fix:** use lands of about 0.5 × pitch and a depth of about 0.5 × pitch.

### 104 (low; open since p104)
- **Flaw:** the worm-wheel teeth are smoothed across creases up to 71° (facet screen: 1,836 edges), giving sheen streaks on the flanks.
- **Capture:** `facet.json`, `tiles/104.png`.
- **Fix:** crease-split the normals at about 30° in the worm-wheel generator.

### 107 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the serpentine groove shows about 5 turn-arounds on the visible face, against about 8 on the plate.
- **Capture:** `tiles/107.png`.
- **Fix:** use 8–10 strokes per revolution if the follower allows it; otherwise record the count as forced.

### 111 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the thread run-outs still end in thin tapering fins that poke past the ends of the orange sleeve and the blue screw tip. All three threads are the same hand (crest analysis), as captioned ("run in different directions" refers to pitch direction relative to the nut).
- **Capture:** `tiles/111.png` (top, below).
- **Fix:** in the shared helical-thread helper, end each thread square, or with a short radial ramp, flush with the end face.

### 116 (medium; open since p104)
- **Flaw:** the rear pawl and the rear pinion are both #7e8584. From behind, the pawl disappears into the pinion's face; only an outline and its pin show, so the rear rectifier's working pawl can't be read.
- **Capture:** `z/116a.png` (panel 3 of `z/colour.png`).
- **Fix:** colour both pawls brass or dark steel (not grey or gold, since the ratchets are gold).

### 117 (low; open since p101)
- **Flaw:** the yoke is still a busy all-blue cage of rails, collars, nuts and forks that hides the cam and rollers.
- **Capture:** `tiles/117.png`.
- **Fix:** make each side one flat rail plate, keep the two-cheek forks, and give the rails or forks a contrasting tone.

### 118 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the fixed rack base still has a square, stepped left end against a sloped right end. Brown rounds both.
- **Capture:** `tiles/118.png`.
- **Fix:** mirror the right end onto the left end.

### 123 (low; open since p101)

- **Capture:** `tiles/123.png`, `tiles/123-cycle.png`.

### 125 (low; open since p101)

### 128 (low; open since p93)

### 140 (low; open since p104)

### 149 (medium; open since p104)

### 151 (low; open since p101)

### 154 (medium; new)
- **Flaw:** the weighted cord hangs on the free end of a bare black stub pin that stands about 0.2 in front of the gold lever:
  - lever z 0.36–0.56, cord z 0.684–0.756.
  - Seen from the side, the cord passes beside the lever, not through its eye.
  - Brown loops the cord round the lever's end eye, in the lever's plane.
  - This is the "cheating pin/protrusion not in the engraving" class, and the cord doesn't attach where drawn.
- **Capture:** `z/154side.png` (panel 4 of `z/colour.png`), `z/154m.png`, `bbox.mjs 154 0`.
- **Fix:**
  - Give the lever tip a round eye, and move the lever (or the cord and top pulley) into one plane, so the cord ends in a small loop through that eye.
  - Alternatively, bring the pulley plane back to the lever's mid-plane (z ≈ 0.46) and shorten the pin to the eye's thickness.

### 164 (low; open since p90)
