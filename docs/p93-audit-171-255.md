## Sub-lane A: 171–191

Captures: sheets `/dev/shm/p93/c/sheets/NNN.png` (plate, def, zoom 2.2× at ph .15, left −50, right +50, top 70, behind, ph .33, ph .66); zooms and 8-phase strips in `/dev/shm/p93/c/171/`.

### 174 — high
- The jaws never turn: over a full cycle jaw0 rotates 0.0007 rad and jaw1 0.0002 rad (probe `171/probe.mjs`), so the bar just slides in and out of a fixed throat with visible clearance above and below it. The caption says pushing the piece in "made [the jaws] to turn on the screws and clamp the sides". Capture: `171/strip-174.png`, `171/zb.png` (174-tail). Fix: drive the jaws from the bar's contact (or prescribe it) so each turns several degrees about its screw as the bar enters, with the hooked ends closing onto the bar's faces and the tails crossing further, as drawn.
- (low) The backboard's plank joints are still open slits: from behind, the orange bar shows through the seams. Capture: `171/zb.png` (174-back). Fix: close the joints (0 gap) and keep the seam as a shallow groove or a colour line.

### 180 — high
- The pivoted jaw is completely static (rotation range 0 over the cycle), and the board slides up and down in a gap without ever touching the jaw's curl, so nothing clamps. Capture: `171/strip-180.png`, `sheets/180.png`. Fix: as for 174, turn the jaw about its screw as the board is pushed in, so the curl bears on the board, and back off as it is withdrawn.
- (low) The board still ends flat below the cam and in some phases hangs below the side-piece, where the plate breaks it off. Capture: `171/strip-180.png`. Fix: lengthen the board so its lower end stays off-frame (or below the side-piece) through the stroke.

### 183, 184 — medium
- The quadrant and wing window outlines are wobbly hand traces: the lower quadrant's web opening has lumpy, asymmetric sides and a kink, and the wing's inner edge is bumpy (faceting screen: wing 17 creased edges up to 18.7°, quadrant 16 up to 15.9°, upper arm up to 23°). Brown intends concentric arcs and straight radial webs. Capture: `171/zc.png` (top-left and top-middle), `sheets/183.png`. Fix: rebuild each quadrant/wing as arcs concentric with its shaft plus straight radial web edges, with fillets, keeping the working rims unchanged.

### 185 — medium

### 190 — medium

- (low) The swivelling pressure shoe has the same colour as the work block, so it reads as a pointed roof on the block and "detaches" from it when the holder lifts. Capture: `171/zb.png` (190-nose5). Fix: give the shoe the holder's or a steel colour.
