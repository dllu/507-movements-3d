## Medium findings

### 429: pointed piston arms → broad, square-ended arms with packing boxes

- **Verified.** Brown's two long pistons per rotor are parallel-sided arms. Each ends square in a packing box on the casing. The generated rotors tapered from 17° at the base to 5° at the tip, which read as diamond spikes.
- **Change.** In `scripts/generate-holly-mate.py`, each piston is now:
  - a constant-width arm, 1.44 source units wide (0.36 of the pitch radius);
  - ending in a face concentric with the bore, 0.009 inside it;
  - with corners rounded by 0.10.
- The packing slot and strip grew from 0.20 × 0.32 to 0.46 × 0.56, matching Brown's boxes.
- The mate's recesses are regenerated by the same conjugate sweep. `generated-holly-mate.js` was regenerated, and so was `docs/validation/429-mating-contact.json`, through `export-holly-contact.mjs` and `review-holly-contact.py`:
  - 1025 interleaved poses;
  - 0 penetrating poses and 0 overlap;
  - gaps from 0.0072 to 0.0122.
- **Captures:**
  - `429-after.png`: the plate, phases 0 and 0.33, rotated, and a zoom.
  - `429-phases.png`: phases 0.2, 0.45 and 0.7, and the left view.
- **Tests.** `movement-429` and `holly-mating-profile` pass.
- **Loop seams.** The steam reshape pop at about phase 0.38 was already there at HEAD (36.97% before, 35.78% now).
- **Unchanged residual.** Brown draws both rotors with their arms at about 65°. The conjugate phasing puts them at 90° and 0° at t = 0. That rest pose was not changed.
- **Proposed row.**
  - assessment: reasonable
  - visibleFlaws: ""
  - limits: replace the p90 clause with "p96: generated conjugate rotors: rounded U-rooted teeth and two broad constant-width pistons per rotor ending square on the bore with Brown's packing boxes (1025 poses, 0 overlap, gaps 0.007–0.012); teeth shallower than Brown's; the rest pose is the conjugate phasing (arms vertical and horizontal), not Brown's 65°."
