# Pass 93 audit, lane p93-e: movements 341–425

- **Reviewer:** Claude Opus 5.5, lane p93-e. Forked sub-lanes could not start because the concurrent-subagent limit was reached, so this lane reviewed all 85 movements itself.
- **Date:** 2026-09-28.
- **Scope:** read-only. No source, test or ledger edits.

## Method

- **Captures:** all 85 movements were captured on a private vite server (port 46393) with `/dev/shm/p93/e/shots.mjs`.
  - Each movement has default, left (yaw −55°, pitch 15°), right (yaw +55°, pitch 15°), top (pitch 65°), behind (yaw 180°), and phase 0.33 and 0.67 views, in `/dev/shm/p93/e/cap/<id>-{d,L,R,T,B,p1,p2}.png`.
  - `/dev/shm/p93/e/tiles/<id>.png` puts the plate beside all seven views. Every tile was inspected against the plate.
  - Zooms are in `/dev/shm/p93/e/z/`. All capture paths below are relative to `/dev/shm/p93/e/`.
- **Screens:** five screens were run over 341–425 into `/dev/shm/p93/e/scr/`: `disc.json`, `cf.json`, `white.json`, `edge.json` and `facet.json`. The merged per-ID leads are in `/dev/shm/p93/e/leads.md`.
  - Leads were verified visually or dismissed.
  - Most "short-of-pin" leads are bore clearances or rim/boss pairs, not visible gaps.
  - The coincident-face pairs flagged in 346, 361, 375, 401 and 420 either hide behind a boss or have contrast < 0.03, and none is visible in zooms.
- **Ledger:** each row's `visibleFlaws` and `limits` was read. Documented flaws are re-reported only where they are fixable. Several p90 findings are confirmed fixed: 353 wipers widened, 360 has 20 teeth and a curved pawl, 365 rod feed cue, 373 pulley in front, 378 bow-saw, 383 spoked brush, 386 plugs, 391 C lever, 392 crank plate, 395 sealed ring, 398 groove, 401 volute, 403 rule widths, 407 flat lath, 409 legs, 415 lever and pawls, 419 wheel A, 424 see-through C.
