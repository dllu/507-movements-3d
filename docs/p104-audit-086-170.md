## Findings (ordered by ID)

### 87 (low; open since p93/p101)
- **Flaw 1:** the gold reversing stud on wheel E still straddles the band's inner edge, so its boss overhangs the spoke window (edge ratio 0.65).
- **Flaw 2:** the rear input shaft's end face is coplanar with the two hidden spokes' faces (flicker screen, contrast 0.52).
- **Capture:** `z/s87a.png`, `z/s87b.png`, `cf.json`, `edge.json`.
- **Fix:**
  - Move the stud to the band's mid-radius, or shrink its boss to about 0.8× the band width, and re-time the strike on G.
  - End the shaft 0.005 inside the hub.

### 90, 91 (low; open since p90)
- **Flaw 1:** the yoke stems are still round rods. Brown draws flat bars filleted into the yoke.
- **Flaw 2:** 91's frame is still not level. Its top edge falls 5 px over 260 px and its bottom edge 2 px (measured in `091/def.png`), so the frame reads as slightly skewed and trapezoidal.
- **Capture:** `tiles/090.png`, `tiles/091.png`.
- **Fix:** make the stems a rectangular section in the yoke extrusion, with fillets. Rebuild 91's outline from level, mirror-symmetric straight edges and arcs.

### 99 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the guide rails are still thin wire posts that end free at both ends, reached from the grey block only by a bracket behind it.
- **Capture:** `z/s99.png`, `tiles/099.png`.
- **Fix:** thicken the rails to about 0.16, and make the block and brackets one extrusion that reaches the rails.

### 102, 103 (low; open since p90)
- **Flaw:** the square threads are still deep, thin fins with lands of about ⅓ of the pitch. 102 has a thread depth of 0.111 on a 0.275 core.
- **Capture:** `tiles/102.png`, `tiles/103.png`.
- **Fix:** use lands of about 0.5 × pitch and a depth of about 0.5 × pitch.

### 104 (low; new)
- **Flaw:** the throated worm-wheel teeth are smoothed across creases up to 71° (facet screen: 1,836 edges). In the default zoom this shows as grey sheen streaks on the tooth flanks, and in side view as wavy, uneven tooth faces.
- **Capture:** `z/r104.png` (panels 1–2), `facet.json`.
- **Fix:** split normals at a crease angle of about 30° in the worm-wheel generator (`toCreasedNormals`), or regenerate the throat with more axial samples.

### 107 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the serpentine groove shows about 5 turn-arounds on the visible face, against about 8 on the plate.
- **Capture:** `tiles/107.png`.
- **Fix:** use 8–10 strokes per revolution if the follower allows it; otherwise document the count as forced.

### 111 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the thread run-outs still end in thin tapering fins that poke past both ends of the orange sleeve and the blue screw tip. The handedness matches the plate.
- **Capture:** `z/111.png`.
- **Fix:** in the shared helical-thread helper, end each thread square, or with a short radial ramp, flush with the end face.

### 116 (low; new)
- **Flaw:** the rear pawl and the rear pinion are the same grey (#7e8584). From behind, the pawl vanishes into the pinion's face and only its pin shows.
- **Capture:** `z/116d.png`, `z/116e.png`.
- **Fix:** colour both pawls brass or steel-dark, distinct from both pinions.

### 117 (low; open since p101)
- **Flaw:** the yoke is still a busy all-blue cage of rails, collars, nuts and forks. It hides the cam and rollers in the default and rotated views.
- **Capture:** `tiles/117.png`.
- **Fix:**
  - Make each side one flat rail plate spanning the depth.
  - Keep the two-cheek forks.
  - Give the rails or forks a contrasting tone.

### 118 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the fixed rack base still has a square, stepped left end against a sloped/rounded right end. Brown rounds both.
- **Capture:** `tiles/118.png`.
- **Fix:** mirror the right end onto the left end.

### 123 (low; open since p101)

- **Capture:** `tiles/123-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** colour the centre gear brass, and the spur gears behind the sectors grey or gold.

### 125 (low; open since p101)

- **Capture:** `tiles/125-cycle.png`.

### 128 (low; open since p93)

- **Capture:** `tiles/128.png`, `facet.json`.

### 140 (low; new)
- **Flaw 1:** the hand lever's fixed shaft (x ±0.07) sits centred on the rear frame's side edge (x max 0.010). Behind the pedestal (z −0.26…−0.15) half of it pokes out beside the thin rear plate, which is visible from behind.

- **Capture:** `z/140.png` (panels 1–2), `tiles/140.png` (back).

### 143 (low; open since p101)

### 147 (low; open since p96)

### 149 (low; new)

### 151 (low; open since p101)

### 164 (low; open since p90)
