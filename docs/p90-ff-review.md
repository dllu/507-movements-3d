## Highs

### 462: chain pump
- **Wheels.** Brown's wheels are open stars: a small hub and eight long arms. The chain beads sit on a thin circle between the arms.
  - Each cheek is now one flat plate (0.05 thick): hub, eight arms reaching r 0.82 set between the pockets, and a thin rim (r 0.54–0.665) with the swept carrier-shaft pockets.
  - Generated by `scripts/generate-chain-pump-pins.mjs` into `src/simulation/chain-pump-pin-profiles.js`. The arms lose no area to the pin sweep.
  - The separate 0.006-thick spoke boxes, which lay coplanar with the rim plate and caused the speckle, are gone.
- **Arm length.** It is capped by clearance: the riser foot is 0.75 above the lower axle, and the spout end wall is under the upper wheel. So the arms reach 1.32 × the chain radius, where Brown's reach about 1.6 ×.
- **Cheek position.** The inner face is at z 0.2725, clear of the disks (z ≤ 0.27). The shafts stay 0.56 long, because they must pass the riser bore.
- **Bank.** The receiving flume is now the top of one solid bank, a U-section extruded along x, standing on the pool floor. Its foot runs 0.01 below the floor, so it is not coplanar with the water's bottom. The closed end wall runs into the floor and the side walls, with its top 0.005 below theirs. The sheet leaving the open end falls into the pool beyond the bank.
- **Captures:** `cap/462/tile.png`, `z/462m.png` (wheel close, rotated wheel, bank).
- **Screens:**
  - Coincident faces: 0.
  - Seams: 0.
  - Body intersections: only the pass-49 chain/cross-shaft value (0.0398).
  - Disconnected parts: the bank group is reported floating, because the pool floor is not a mesh (it stands on `groundY`, like the pump). The riser/spout near-miss is unchanged.
- **Tests:** movement-462 (11) and chain-weir-interfaces (3) pass. The drive gap is 0.0039 (min and max).
- **Shared helper.** `chain-weir-working-parts.js` also serves 463. Its geometry hash is unchanged (`95af1bd513ca7fd4` before and after).
