# Pass 96 audit, lane p96-e: movements 341–425

- **Reviewer:** Claude Opus 5.5, lane p96-e. Four forked sub-lanes covered 341–357 (e1), 358–374 (e2), 375–391 (e3) and 392–408 (e4). A fifth fork could not start because the concurrent-subagent limit was reached, so the lane parent audited 409–425 (e5) itself. The parent spot-checked the high finding (376) and the mediums for 358 and 407.
- **Date:** 2026-09-28, at HEAD 7ce1e83.
- **Scope:** read-only. No source, test, ledger or git changes.

## Method

- **Captures:** a private vite server on port 46961 served all 85 movements, captured with `/dev/shm/p96/e/shots.mjs`.
  - Each movement has eleven views in `/dev/shm/p96/e/cap/<id>-<v>.png`:
    - `d`: default.
    - `L` and `R`: yaw ∓50°, pitch 15°.
    - `T`: top, pitch 65°.
    - `B`: behind.
    - `U`: below, pitch −50°.
    - `p1`–`p4`: phases 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8.
    - `Lp`: yaw −35° at phase 0.5.
  - `/dev/shm/p96/e/tiles/<id>.png` puts the plate beside all eleven views. Every tile was inspected against the plate.
  - Movements with a top-down or flat default view got extra tilted views, because yaw alone only spins them. For 412–425 these are in `f5/<id>-tilt.png`.
  - Motion strips (8 to 12 phases) were made wherever there are linkages or contacts, including 353, 360, 364, 370, 389, 392, 393, 396, 397, 398, 402 and 420.
  - Zooms are in `/dev/shm/p96/e/f1/`–`f5/`.
  - **All capture paths below are relative to `/dev/shm/p96/e/`.**
- **Screens:** all five screens ran over 341–425 into `/dev/shm/p96/e/scr/`: `disc.json`, `cf.json`, `white.json`, `edge.json` and `facet.json`. The merged per-ID leads are in `/dev/shm/p96/e/leads.md`. Each lead was verified visually or numerically (`bbox.mjs`), or dismissed with a reason (see the end).
- **Ledger:** each row's `visibleFlaws` and `limits` was read, along with p93-audit-341-425, p93-fe, p94-a/b/p and p95. Documented forced flaws are re-reported only where a concrete fix exists.
