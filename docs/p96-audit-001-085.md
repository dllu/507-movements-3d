# Pass 96 audit, movements 1–85 (lane p96-a)

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, lane p96-a (third comprehensive pass). The lane was split into four forked sub-lanes: p96-a1 (1–21), p96-a2 (22–43), p96-a3 (44–64) and p96-a4 (65–85). Their sections are merged below. Date: 2026-09-28. This was a read-only audit of the working tree at `7ce1e83`. Other lanes modified files under `src/` while this audit ran; the captures were taken at the start of the audit. Nothing under `src/`, `tests/` or `docs/` was edited apart from this file, and no git writes were made.

## Method

- **Server:** a vite server on port 47383 served the working tree. Captures were made with Playwright (`chromium.launch({channel:'chrome', headless:true})`) using `/dev/shm/p96/a/shots.mjs`, which supports `pick`, `focus`, `abs`, `hide` and `roles` targets.
- **Views sheets:** one per movement, at `/dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/<id>-views.png`. Each has eight tiles: the plate, default, right (yaw +50°, pitch 20°), left (yaw −50°, pitch −15°), top (pitch 75°), behind (yaw 180°), rear oblique (yaw 130°, pitch 30°) and below (pitch −65°).
- **Phase sheets:** one per movement, at `/dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/<id>-phases.png`, with eight phases at the default view (steps of 0.125). These were used to check motion over the whole cycle: hovering, jumps, direction, and engagement.
- **Singles and zooms:** the single shots are in `/dev/shm/p96/a/sh/`; the zooms and montages are in `/dev/shm/p96/a/z/` (`a1-*` to `a4-*`, one prefix per sub-lane).
- **Screens:** all five ran over 1–85, with output in `/dev/shm/p96/a/`:
  - `disc.json`: `screen-disconnected-parts.mjs`
  - `cf.json`: `screen-coincident-faces.mjs` (026 was rerun alone, giving `cf-a2-26.json`)
  - `white.json`: `screen-white-parts.mjs`
  - `edge.json`: `screen-edge-mounts.mjs`
  - `facet.json`: `screen-faceting.mjs`
- **Ledger:** every row for 1–85 in `docs/movement-status.json` was read, along with docs/p93-audit-001-085.md and the p93-f*, p94 and p95 reviews. Documented forced flaws are only repeated where a fix looks available.

## Findings, 1–21 (sub-lane p96-a1)

Every movement in 1–21 was checked on its views sheet (plate, default, right, left, top, back, back-oblique, below) and its phase sheet (eight phases, default view), with zooms on the joints, hubs, ropes and belt ends.

### 004 — medium
- The rope leaving the capstan runs diagonally from the top coil down to the idler height, and it passes through the middle and bottom coils on the right side of the drum. From the right, the strands visibly merge into one another, so rope clips through rope. The ledger covers only axial slip, not this.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a1-m3.png (left: view from +90° yaw; the diagonal strand cuts through two coils), /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a1-4capz.png, /dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/4-views.png.
- Fix: route each strand so it leaves the drum tangentially from the coil at its own idler's height. The incoming strand should wind onto the bottom coil, and the outgoing strand should leave from the coil level with the other idler. Alternatively, set the two idlers at the heights of the end coils, so that no free span has to cross the helix. Keep the helix pitch at least one rope diameter plus clearance.

- Fix: raise the boss cylinder to 48–64 radial segments, or build it with the shared smooth hub builder used for the cast-ring hubs.

## Findings, 22–43 (sub-lane p96-a2)

Every view sheet (`/dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/<id>-views.png`: the plate, default, right, left, top, behind, rear oblique and below) and every eight-phase sheet (`<id>-phases.png`) was read. Zooms are in `/dev/shm/p96/a/z/a2-*.png`. The montages are `a2-m1.png` to `a2-m15.png`.

### 028 — medium
- During every 1.2 s adjustment stage, the roller and its whole shaft rise 0.2 clear of the disc. Around phases 0.60–0.66 the roller hovers in the air, then snaps inward and drops back. This is an invented motion: the caption ("changing the distance of the upper wheel from the center") and the plate imply that the wheel slides along its shaft while it stays on the disc. The code is `liftHeight = 0.2` in `brushWheels()` in `src/simulation/authored-gears-core.js`, around line 1160.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a2-m4.png (phases 0.50–0.75; the gap shows in the 0.60–0.64 tiles), /dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/28-phases.png (row 2, column 2).
- Fix: set `liftHeight` to 0. Keep the roller shaft fixed in its bearings, and translate only the roller along the shaft (a keyed sliding fit) while it stays in contact. The driver may keep its stop during the shift; with no lift, it reads as a brush-wheel adjustment.

### 029 — low
- The spur's teeth were re-cut against the rib in p93, and their flanks now have visible stair-stepped, striated facets (horizontal hatch lines and torn edges at the roots). They show in the right view at 4× and plainly at 7×.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a2-m5.png (panels 1–2), /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a2-m14.png (bottom left).
- Fix: compute the re-cut 2D tooth envelope once, with dense sampling, then fit or simplify it into a clean polyline or spline. Build the spur as one smooth extrusion of that outline, not as a swept-cutter mesh.

### 022 — low
- The S-hook between the lowest pulley's frame and the weight eye ends in an uncapped tube. The disconnected-parts screen reports an open end at (−0.58, −1.80, 0). The tip is a flat, square cut, where the rulebook asks for hooks with capped, rounded ends (as on 352).
- Capture: /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a2-m13.png (panels 1–2).
- Fix: close the tube's free end with a hemispherical cap of the tube's radius, or taper it to a rounded point.

### 025 — low

## Findings, 44–64 (sub-lane p96-a3)

Every movement had its views sheet (`/dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/<id>-views.png`: the plate, then default, right, left, top, back, back-oblique and below) and its phase sheet (`<id>-phases.png`: eight phases at the default view) checked against the plate. Zooms are in `/dev/shm/p96/a/z/a3-*.png`.

### 046 — medium
- The fusee is stepped into about six flat tiers, but the chain is wound on it as a smooth helix. So the chain's turns cross the tier edges and hover in the air between the treads instead of sitting in a groove. The worst of this is the upper front turn in mid-cycle.
- Low: the shaft's shadow on the barrel wall and inside the barrel is a jagged, stair-stepped line (shadow-map aliasing).
- Capture: /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a3-46-m.png (left and middle panels: phase 0.375; right panel: the barrel), /dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/46-phases.png.
- Fix: build the fusee as one smooth lathed solid with the classic concave-conical profile, and cut a shallow spiral groove whose pitch and radius law match the chain helix, so the chain sits in the groove. Alternatively, keep the tiers but make the chain step from tier to tier. For the shadow, raise `shadowCameraHalfExtent` or the map size for 46, or add a small normal bias.

### 048 — medium
- The driven gear is about two-thirds of the plate's size. On the plate its diameter is about 11.3 shaft diameters; in the model it is about 7.6 (the clutch body, at about 4 shaft diameters, matches). The gear also carries coarse teeth, where the plate draws many fine ones.
- The pinion sits directly above the gear, so it stands up past the gear's top edge. On the plate, its edge-on outline (the small hub rectangle) lies inside the gear's outline, about 60° down from the top. That means the plate's pinion meshes on the viewer's side of the gear, not at its top.
- Capture: /dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/48-views.png (the plate beside the default view), /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a3-48-m.png.
- Fix: scale the gear up to about 11 shaft diameters and raise its tooth count to keep a fine module (keep the mesh ratio by scaling the pinion's count too). Move the pinion's centre around the gear to about 60° from the top, towards the camera, so the default view matches the plate.

### 052 — medium

- Capture: /dev/shm/p96/a/sheets/52-phases.png (compare the shaft's left end at phases 0, 0.25 and 0.375), /dev/shm/p96/a/z/a3-52-m.png.
- Fix: parent the axial slide only to the sliding disk, its hub and its collar, keep the shaft and the end barrel axially fixed, and add a feather key on the shaft as in 47 and 48.

### 063 — medium

Slim the bracket and move it behind the drop's plane, so the default view shows only the spring and the small stop pin, as on the plate.
