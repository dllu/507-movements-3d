## 269: closed end joggled behind the pinion (`authored-mutilated-racks.js`)

m15 found that rolling the last lower pair carries the gear's tips a tip radius (about 3.1
pitches) past the last tooth, while Brown closes the frame 0.25 pitch past it. A closed end in
the gear's plane therefore had to sit about 3 pitches farther right than drawn. The frame's
relieved-tooth webs already use the layer behind the pinion, and this lane uses it for the
closed end too.

- **Bridge:** the right bridge now sits behind the pinion's rear face (z −0.30…−0.42; the gear's
  rear face is at −0.23). Its inner face is Brown's 0.25 pitch past the last tooth. Its width is
  Brown's 48 px, which is 2.36 pitches (0.726; it was 0.45).
- **Joggles:** each rail's end steps back to the bridge's depth through a joggle block. The block
  lies at |y| ≥ the rack root line, outside the gear's tip circle.
- **Rod:** the round rod starts 0.04 past the gear tips' reach at the stroke limit. Between the
  bridge and the rod, the rod's flat end is also joggled back (dark, rod-height, at the bridge's
  depth). From the front it reads as the rod leaving the closed end, where Brown draws it. The
  rod's far end and the collar keep the same place relative to the closed end.
- **Stroke limit:** the gear tips now pass in front of the bridge and the rod's flat end. This
  matches the front view on the plate.
- **Hub:** the rear of the hub stops at −0.27, because it overhangs the bridge at the stroke limit.
  The front boss is unchanged.
- **Shaft:** the shaft clears the bridge's inner face by 0.017 at the stroke limit and now runs
  back to −0.56.
- **Bearing parts:** these are undrawn and hidden by source presentation. The torus moved to
  z −0.50, and the post and foot moved to −0.67, all behind the bridge.
- **Frame length:** the frame is 1.4 pitches shorter (frameRight 3.414, was 4.101).
- **Framing:** `cameraFitBounds` is now computed from the frame's swept silhouette. It runs from
  the open end at one stroke limit to the closed end at the other (root scale 0.92, ±0.01):
  x −4.857…5.553, where it was the fixed −4.9…6.2. The subject grows by about 6.6%: the gear is
  about 130 px across on the stage (it was 122), so a pitch renders at about 20.6 px against
  Brown's 20.3 px. As before, only the rod leaves the view at the stroke limit (maxNdc 1.16 →
  1.18), which is where Brown breaks it off.
- **What remains:** at the source pose the closed end's inner face is 6.25 pitches right of the
  gear centre, where Brown has 4.6 (it was 9.4). The model's four groups run on one 17-pitch
  line; Brown's upper-seven and lower-two groups overlap in x. The relief also still needs that

- **Captures:** default, oblique, stroke-limit (t 6.38 and 5.8) and mid-stroke (2.38, 4) stills
  were inspected. At the stroke limit the gear passes over the bridge, and the bridge and rails

- **Intersections:** clear → clear (2 bodies, no pairs).
- **Tests:** the source-proportion test now checks the 0.25-pitch inner face and the 48 px width,

  joggled closed end", samples 193 poses. At each one it checks that no pinion mesh's box meets

## 76: crop to Brown's broken-off wheel (`jointed-tappet.js`)

m16 fitted the section view to segment D's whole ±2.38 orbit. That left the subject at about 62%
of the plate's scale. Brown draws only a broken piece of the large wheel, and u6 already clips
it with section planes that turn with the wheel. The section view now crops where Brown crops.

- **Change:** in the 'section' view, `cameraFitBounds` is the union of two boxes. The first holds
  every non-driver part over one displayed period (ratchet A's tip circle, both pawls, the tappet
  and its dog, the fixed pins): x −1.001…2.328, y −1.001…1.202. These values were measured offline
  from vertices at 769 poses, and a test recomputes them. The second is segment D and its stud at
  the plate pose, computed from the section planes: x 1.529…2.384, y −1.367…1.036. The result is
  x −1.001…2.384, y −1.367…1.202, where it was ±2.384 square. The 'complete' view keeps the whole
  disc.
- **Result:**
  - A now spans about 0.54 of the stage width, against 0.55 of the plate on the engraving (it was
    about 0.38).
  - The segment's broken ends fall at about 20% and 85% of the stage height, where Brown's fall
    at 15% and 84%.
  - The segment turns clockwise with the wheel. It leaves at the bottom edge about 1 s into the
    12 s cycle and passes out of view round the left and top. Its spoke and arc cross the edges
    until it comes back in from the top right. maxNdc is 0.92 → 1.67, from stud D alone.
- **Checked:** A, the holding pawl, the tappet and dog, and every fixed pin stay inside the view
  at all 193 sampled poses. Only the driver (segment, spoke and stud) leaves. No motion or geometry
  changed, so nothing was rebaked.
- **Load cost:** the crop adds only a 2880-angle plane test at construction. An in-factory motion
  sweep was tried and dropped, because it added about 150 ms.
- **Captures:** default and stills at 0.6, 0.84, 1.5, 3, 4.5, 6, 7.5, 9 and 10.5 s were inspected.
- **Intersections:** unchanged, as the change is display-only. The screen shows 6 bodies with no solid pairs. What it does list: the display caps on the driver's cut faces (0.050 and 0.027, same as u6), and the coaxial dog stop pin at zero depth.
- **Tests:** a new test, "engraving section frames A, the pawls and the tappet throughout,
  cropping only segment D as Brown does". jointed-tappet: 9/9.
