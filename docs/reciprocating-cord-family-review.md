# Reciprocating cord, treadle and saw: 359, 374, 392

Reviewed the official [359](https://507movements.com/mm_359.html), [374](https://507movements.com/mm_374.html), and [392](https://507movements.com/mm_392.html) captions and engravings. The 359 page marks animation unavailable. The 374 and 392 pages contain official canvas models; their circle-link and slider-crank constructions agree with the existing analytical laws. Those laws remain unchanged. Source HTML and browser captures are retained only in `/dev/shm`.

## Corrections

- **359 pump drill:** the analytic cord radius now represents the cord centreline, with room for its finite thickness around the narrower visible spindle. The sliding hand bar receives a closed, bored sleeve rather than a torus. Both rope branches retain capped geometry buffers during playback; material-marker spheres are hidden to expose the cord. The original smooth alternating rotation and hand-bar displacement remain.
- **374 treadle eccentric:** real recessed channels replace the generic pulley rims, at the actual belt plane. The eccentric disk has its source offset shaft connection, while the treadle roller has a true axle bore. Fixed shaft/fulcrum journals and the moving treadle web have actual pin bores. Axles span their journals, and the standards meet journal undersides without filling the bores. Face marks retain visible rotation. The source-derived constant belt length, circle constraints and ideal no-slip ratio remain.
- **392 gig saw:** a rigid bored connecting rod receives the crank and wrist pins on a separate front plane; extended pins provide actual axial engagement. Fixed guide cheeks cover the sliding blocks' depth with running side clearance, and rear standards connect them. The table slot clears the blade's actual tooth width. A bored crank journal and shaft meet the shortened support standard. The exact reconstructed slider-crank law and the official animation's tangent-circle spring path remain.

All three use source-facing views, suppress ground/fog, and enforce readable minimum display periods of 8, 9, and 5 seconds. Initial browser review found no errors or cycle clipping (maximum NDC extents .730, .843 and .902). The final 374 review covers its restored face marks and corrected solid eccentric center (.841 NDC). A local 120-update measurement averaged approximately .313/.014/.039 ms per update; these measurements are hardware-specific. Playback retains scene size and geometry buffers.

## Evidence and limits

The unchanged legacy movement tests and `tests/reciprocating-cord-working-solids.test.mjs` passed 32/32 checks. After the final rod stand-off correction, all 11 affected 392 checks passed. The eight focused checks also cover both 359 cord branches against one another and the 374 treadle against its fixed standard, and the 392 rod against the nearby guide cheeks with both pins spanning the full rod-eye depth. Selected rendered-surface sweeps check 17 complete-cycle poses in both directions. Dynamic cord vertices and triangle BVHs are refreshed at every pose. Further positive controls establish groove floors and retaining lips, real journal/rod bores, guide depth, table slot width, standard/journal material overlap, and retained buffers.

This is selected-interface qualification, not a global collision or force solve. For 359, the common tied anchorage and connector transition above `anchorY - connectorDrop - .05` are excluded from clearance qualification; that terminal region still uses a prescribed knot/connector reconstruction. The smooth change between opposite winding directions is retained as a kinematic approximation. Flywheel energy, hand force and cord tension are not dynamically solved. For 374, foot force, stored inertia, compliance, friction and loaded belt slip remain idealized. For 392, the spring's positive preload is a display proxy derived from its prescribed path length, not an elastic force solution; cutting forces, blade vibration and material removal are omitted. The added supports, pin stand-offs and running clearances are inferred 3D details. No MuJoCo contact or force validation is claimed.
