## Notes and honest residuals

- 455: `old-pump-contact-profile.js` was regenerated by the updated `scripts/generate-old-pump-contact.mjs`.
  It checks vane vertices inside the block, block vertices inside the vane, and distances both ways.
  The block stops 0.002 short of the wall, where the sealing lips run, and the rear cover carries it.
  Results: maximum fold 107.4 deg, peak at 125.7 deg of travel. The minimum closing hinge moment arm is
  0.639 and the minimum sample gap is 0.00015. The hold and quintic return are unchanged (145-178 deg).
  As the folded tip slips past the square corner, in the last few degrees of closing, the push gives a
  small forward shaft moment (24 of 366 engaged samples, minimum -0.26). The contact stays compressive.
  `tests/old-pump-455-contact.test.mjs` was rewritten for the polygon block.
  `docs/old-rotary-pump-455-contact-review.md` still describes the round lobe and is superseded here.
- 456: the flanks are harmonic, so acceleration steps at the dwell ends, as a heart cam's does.
  The chamber wall is the envelope of the sealing heads plus 0.003 running clearance. It meets the drum
  at E.
- 459: the buckets now start and stop with the take-up (velocity steps). A worm taking up a stopped
  wheel must do this, and it replaces the wind wheel stopping. The old C2 stop tests were replaced with
  tests for the constant wind rate, the tappet-bail contact while pushing, both pin rings clearing the
  thread in the free window, and a C1 selector. The engagement shift is larger, so the worm swings
  about 6 deg.
- 467 and 472 need proportion rebuilds, not residual fixes.
  - 467: Brown's narrow column has the pump in the ram and a small base.
  - 472: Brown's wide cast frame C carries a small pump D in its base pocket, a small cylinder B at the
    head and an arched anvil pedestal.
  Both were left unchanged.
- 502, 504 and 507: each carrier or arm turns fully, and Brown does not crop the train, so the frame
  must hold the whole sweep. The fits now follow the actual swept surfaces, not per-mesh boxes, with a
  shallow depth proxy for the near-orthographic views. The trains are about 7-20% larger. They still
  read small or off-centre beside the plate.
  - 507: E's 61 teeth give about Brown's hatch density. Edge-on, they read as low-contrast serrations.
- `docs/validation/503-504-contact-solids.json` was regenerated because it fingerprints
  `authored-epicyclic-trains.js` and `epicyclic-family-corrections.js`. The results are unchanged; only
  the hashes differ.
- Display-profile motion bounds are stale for 410, 443, 455, 456, 459, 483, 485, 502, 504 and 507.
  The integrator needs to re-measure them.
