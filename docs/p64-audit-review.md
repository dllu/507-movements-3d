# Pass 64 rotated-view audits of the older rows

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5 (read-only audit lanes p64-audit-a, -b and -c), 2026-09-26.

Scope: the 274 movements rated reasonable whose last visual review predated the user's newest rules (no undrawn stands, pillars, back bars, feet, guides, hangers or extensions; no kinks; Brown's intended 2D geometry; no pins or protrusions behind parts; quadrant cues; flat shading; taut laid ropes; see-through foregrounds only where Brown dots hidden workings). Each was compared with its plate in the default view, then inspected rotated about ±60–70° about vertical, from the back and top, and at several motion phases including just before the loop seam. The loop-seam checker ran on all of them.
