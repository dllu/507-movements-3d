## Per movement

### 429 — medium
- The major rotor lobes are pointed spikes. Brown's are broad, square-ended arms with packing boxes (see the table above).
- The small teeth also stay shallower than Brown's; this part is documented.
- Captures: `z/429-cmp.png` (plate, default and phase 0.33), `z/m-a.png` (tiles 1–2), `sheets/429.png`.
- Fix: flat-ended constant-width arms with slotted packing strips, then re-run the conjugate generation.

### 448 — medium
- The handle–link pin sticks out one side by about a handle width.
- The fulcrum pin is an oversized black drum.
- The handle still ends in a black ball.
- Captures: `z/m-e.png` (default zoom, and the +60° and −70° views at phase 0.3).
- Fix: pins of the joint's width with small heads, centred in round eyes.

### 498 — medium
- The zero tag floats in front of the pressure leg, attached to nothing.
- The scale board is ivory (#e6dcc3). It is borderline on the white screen, but it reads acceptably.
- Captures: `z/m-j.png` (tiles 4–5), `sheets/498.png`.
- Fix: carry the tag on a bracket from the board, or on a band round the tube.

### 430 — low
- The headrace flume is still a flat plank with no side walls, and the feed water is a sheet lying on it (p93 finding unfixed).
- Capture: `z/430-flume.png` (in `z/m-b.png`, tile 1).
- Fix: one U-section extrusion for the flume, with the water sheet inside its walls (`authored-overshot-water-wheels.js`).

### 432 — low
- From behind, a light arc seam still follows the breast curve across the flat back face of the masonry (p93 finding unfixed).
- Capture: `z/432-back.png` (in `z/m-b.png`, tile 2).
- Fix: make the back face one polygon, or skip that mesh in `smoothFacetNormals`.

### 433 — low
- Under the runner, a dark cylinder (the deep hub or support ring) hangs below the boards, with nothing carrying it. Brown draws a plain shaft.
- Capture: `z/433-under.png` (in `z/m-b.png`, tile 3).
- Fix: end the hub flush with the board roots and let the shaft run down as a plain stub, as in 435.

### 435 — low

- Capture: `z/435-centre.png` (in `z/m-b.png`, tile 4), and `strip/row-435.png`.
- Fix: replace the wedges with one annular swept sheet, or a single revolved surface with a radial flow texture.

### 436 — low

- Capture: `z/436-chute.png` (in `z/m-b.png`, tile 6).
- Fix: one flat-topped sheet following the chute floor that bends over the lip into the casing water (`authored-jonval-turbines.js`).

### 439 — low

### 445 / 446 — low (one shared model)
