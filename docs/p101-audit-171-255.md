### 183, 184 — medium
- **Upper handle barely moves.** Over the whole cycle the upper valve handle turns only 10° (probe: 0° → −10° at ph .29–.71, then back). The lower handle is thrown 60°, and in 181/182 both handles are thrown about 55°. So the upper valve is never really opened, and the tappet's top-of-stroke contact reads as a twitch.
  - Capture: `183/strip.png`; `rot.mjs 183|184 '^(upper|lower)-handle-hub$'`.
  - Fix: check the intent against the site animation and the 181/182 timing. If both valves work, as in 181, give the upper handle a full throw (about 55°) at the top of the stroke, latched by the lower quadrant's rim, and rebake. If the 10° is intended, document it in the ledger row.

### 179 — medium
- **Pedestal perched on the foundation.** The lever's grey pedestal is a round eye perched on the foundation block by a tiny stub/nib ("a disc perched on a block, tangent at a point").   - Capture: `179/zz.png` (front, −50/+20, +60/−25).
