- **Reviewer:** Claude Opus 5.5, pass-101 audit lane 256–340, 2026-09-28. Four forked sub-lanes covered 257–277, 278–298, 299–319 and 320–340; 256 was checked centrally. Their findings are merged below.
- **Method:** each movement was captured beside its plate in the default view, right (+50°/15°), left (−50°/−15°), top (65° pitch), behind (180°), below (−60° pitch) and phases 0.33 and 0.66. Suspect spots got zooms, and moving mechanisms got 10-phase strips including the loop seam. Each finding was checked against the movement's `docs/movement-status.json` row and the p96–p100 review docs, so documented forced flaws are not re-reported unless a fix is proposed.
- **Screens:** disconnected-parts, coincident-faces, white-parts, edge-mounts and faceting were run over 256–340. Raw output is in `/dev/shm/p101/r256/{disc,cf,white,edge,facet}.json`. The white-parts screen had 0 hits in this range. Screen hits are reported only where they were verified visually.
- **Captures:** paths are relative to `/dev/shm/p101/r256/`. `NNN/tile.png` holds the plate plus the eight views.

**Counts:** 0 high, 3 medium (266, 271, 278) and 36 low entries (some cover a sibling group such as 262/263 or 335/337/338, and one is a second finding on 278).

## 271 — medium
