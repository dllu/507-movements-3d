# Differential screws 260/266 and worm rack 275

Primary references: [260](https://507movements.com/mm_260.html),
[266](https://507movements.com/mm_266.html), and
[275](https://507movements.com/mm_275.html), including their original engravings.
All three official pages mark animation unavailable. The reversible demonstration
schedules and dimensions are reconstructed; no unavailable animation is claimed
as motion evidence.

## Corrections

- **260:** Two common-module spur reductions turn the translating screw and its
  axially fixed nut at different rates. The original odd/odd B–E pair was mounted
  half a tooth out of mesh despite having the correct speed ratio. Its mounting
  phase is now zero. Both pairs use 30-degree involutes (avoiding the small
  ten-tooth pinion's 20-degree undercut problem), with equal base pitch and
  transverse contact ratios **1.322716 / 1.365281**. Feed remains
  `0.48 × inputTurns × (−10/102 + 19/93)`; the output is not independently sped up.
  The screw and nut have complementary closed square-thread solids, replacing
  wire coils and a solid transparent nut. Gear bodies and hubs have real bores.
  Shafts now reach their left journals throughout the excursion. The rotating
  nut has a bored extension through a stationary bored crosshead; shortened
  upright bars leave that passage open.
- **266:** The two shaft threads and both nuts use the same closed thread helper,
  with pitches 0.30 and 0.24 and genuine complementary internal flanks. Bearing
  bevels and collars no longer intrude into the thread crest. Output remains
  **0.06 per turn** while the shaft itself travels 0.30 per turn. The full
  three-turn excursion retains useful engagement in both nuts.
- **275:** Corrected the visible helix handedness to agree with positive Y-axis
  rotation and the descending rack. A solid square-thread worm replaces the
  coil. Rack tooth faces follow the worm helicoid across their finite width,
  with nominal 0.003 axial flank clearance. Travel remains **one rack pitch per
  worm turn**. Extraneous floor, back post, and guide furniture are hidden to
  match the spare source plate. Full-cycle camera bounds retain the entire rack.

The additive `differential-thread-solids.js` helper reuses the existing closed
helical-thread and bored-lathe geometry builders. It constructs moderate analytic
meshes; no browser physics, optimization, contour tracing, or expensive contact
search is added. All materials explicitly disable scene fog. Minimum display
cycles are 18/12/8 seconds respectively; central display profiles can extend them.

## Validation

All 32 focused tests pass. Run:

```sh
POSES=33 node scripts/review-differential-thread-solids.mjs
node --test tests/movement-260.test.mjs tests/movement-266.test.mjs tests/movement-275.test.mjs tests/differential-thread-solids.test.mjs
```

The checked-in [finite-surface report](validation/260-266-275-thread-solids.json)
records bidirectional containment and nearest-surface queries using the actual
rendered working meshes, vertices, edge midpoints and triangle centers. Thirty-three
poses span each complete programmed cycle. Results:

| Movement | Queries | Penetrations | Largest sampled closest gap for continuously engaged pairs |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 260 | 645,272 | 0 | Screw/nut 0.001758; F–D 0.000130; B–E 0.000066 |
| 266 | 663,719 | 0 | Coarse 0.001622; fine 0.001757 |
| 275 | 140,811 | 0 | 0.002964 (interior teeth 0.002419) |

275 teeth entering/leaving the finite worm ends are reported separately; one
nonworking upper tooth never reaches the worm. A negative-control test deliberately
adds a half-turn phase error and detects tooth penetration. Additional checks cover
shaft seating, finite nut-journal clearance through the crosshead and upright bars,
gear base pitches/contact ratios, real bores, thread compatibility, fog flags,
and saved-report source hashes.

Chrome default/front/advanced-phase comparisons against each engraving showed no
page errors or visible tooth clipping. Render totals including shadows were
72 draws / 119,092 triangles (260), 64 / 57,432 (266), and 34 / 27,792 (275).
Review images stay in `/dev/shm`; bulk artifacts are excluded from Git.

## Limits

These are kinematic models of ideal gears and screw constraints, without friction,
elastic deformation, load-dependent backlash, or dynamic self-locking. The square
thread sections, finite face widths, bearing details, pressure angle and mounting
depths are inferred rather than specified by the engravings. Nut engagement is
partly obscured by its opaque housing, as in a real nut. The finite surface audit
is selected working-pair sampling, not a continuous collision proof or complete
all-parts collision study. 275 retains the source's omitted structural mounting;
its bearings/guides are constraint assumptions. Clearances are small reconstruction
allowances, not a manufacturing tolerance specification.
