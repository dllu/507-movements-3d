# Pass 90 audit, movements 1–85 (lane p90-a)

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, lane p90-a (the parent lane took 22–65; forked sub-lanes took 1–21, 66–75 and 76–85). Date: 2026-09-27. Read-only audit of the working tree: nothing under `src/`, `tests/` or `docs/` was edited apart from this file.

## Method

- **Server:** a vite server on port 45301 served the working tree, captured with Playwright (`chromium.launch({channel:'chrome', headless:true})`) using `/dev/shm/p90/a/shots.mjs`.
- **Contact sheets:** one per movement, at `/dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/<id>.png`. Each shows the plate, the default view at phase 0, yaw +50°/pitch 20°, yaw −50°/pitch −15°, a view from behind, phases 0.33 and 0.66, and a side view at phase 0.5. The single shots are in `/dev/shm/p90/a/sh/` and the zooms in `/dev/shm/p90/a/z/`.
- **Screens:**
  - `node scripts/screen-disconnected-parts.mjs --ids=1-85 --jobs=8`, output in `/dev/shm/p90/a/disc.json`.
  - `node scripts/screen-coincident-faces.mjs --ids=1-85 --jobs=8`, output in `/dev/shm/p90/a/cf.json`.
  - Every lead was checked visually. None survived as a visible defect; each section's "Screens" note says why.
- **Ledger:** each finding was checked against its row in `docs/movement-status.json`. Documented forced flaws are reported only where they look fixable (075, 076).

## Findings, 1–21

### 017 — medium
- The middle (yellow) pulley hangs from the top pulley's right strand, but that strand ends on the bare rear stub of a long axle, hidden behind the sheave and about 0.3 out of the mechanism plane. The plate draws the strand in front, running into the hub. Unlike its siblings in 16, 20 and 21, this pulley has no strap frame or eye. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/17-m.png (side), /dev/shm/p90/a/z/17-m2.png (obliques), /dev/shm/p90/a/z/17-plate.png. Fix: give the middle pulley the same stirrup frame and top eye as 16's movable pulleys, and bring it into the rope plane. Tie the strand to that eye above the sheave and shorten the axle to the frame cheeks.

### 009 — low
- The plate draws a short cylindrical land at the large end of the upper cone and at the small end of the lower cone. The model has plain cones. At the shift extreme (ph ≈ 0.30) the belt edge runs to within a few percent of a belt width of the upper cone's rim. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/9.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/9-m.png. Fix: add the drawn lands (short cylinders of end radius) and cap the shift travel so the belt keeps about half a belt width of margin (10 shares the travel code).

### 006 — low
- The sector's two spokes and chord bar are rendered in the lever's black, not the sector's yellow, so they read as part of the lever rather than the sector web. A long black axle also sticks out fore and aft of the hub, though the plate draws only a ring boss. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/6-m.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/6-m2.png. Fix: give the spokes and chord the sector's material (or extrude them with the sector as one piece). Trim the axle to just proud of the hub.

### 001, 002 (and the spoked-wheel family 004, 005, 011) — low
- The plates draw a large ring hub (about a quarter of the wheel radius) and no protruding shaft. The model has a small black boss and black axle stubs sticking out about half a wheel radius on each side, which dominate the oblique views. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/1-m.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/1.png (1yp, 6side). Fix: in the shared spoked-wheel builder, widen the hub to the drawn ring radius in the wheel's own material and cut the axle stubs to about 0.05 proud.

### 013, 016, 018–021 — low
- The load eye rings are sunk into the weight tops, with their lower third buried in the solid and no staple or boss. In 19 the lower hooks sit right on the weight face. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/hooks-m2.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/hooks-m3.png. Fix: seat each ring in a short eye-bolt shank or boss on the weight top (as 12's ceiling eye does) instead of burying the torus.

Screens: disconnected-parts flagged 5's idler lever as floating (it is a free hand lever, as drawn, so no flaw). The TubeGeometry open ends and tube/torus slivers on the hooks in 12–19 are capped and rigid on inspection. The coincident-face screen flagged 7's B-hollow-shaft Lathe/Extrude pair (area 1.2e-3, contrast 0.27), but it showed no visible flicker at the peak in the captures (/dev/shm/p90/a/z/7-cf.png).
Checked: 7's belt shifts between the fast and loose pulleys and stays on the rims at all phases (/dev/shm/p90/a/z/7-m2.png). 14's 4-over-3 sheave count matches the plate. The crossed ropes in 2, 4 and 6 are clean.

Clean: 003, 007, 008, 010, 012, 014, 015

## Findings, 22–65

### 057 — medium
- The internally toothed ring C is an open annulus with no web, arms or hub, and the orbiting intermediate pinion is a loose gear on a short stub with no carrier arm. Both float with nothing tying them to the common centre, which is obvious from behind and in obliques. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/57m.png (ph 0.33, from yaw 155 and yaw 35), /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/57.png (3back). Fix: give the ring a thin web (or 4 filleted spokes from the shared spoked-wheel builder) on its rear face, bored onto a sleeve on the main shaft. Add one flat carrier arm behind the gears, free on the main shaft, whose outer boss holds the pinion's stub, so the pinion's orbit ("around the common center", per the caption) reads as driven.

### 061, 062 — medium
- The bevel differential that is the whole point of these plates is completely enclosed in opaque pulley shells. Brown draws it with dotted hidden lines inside the pulleys (61: the dashed box and diagonals at the middle and right pulleys; 62: the dashed bevel outlines in the lower pulleys). The standing rule (63, 70, 71 "and the others") is to make the occluding part semi-transparent in the shared see-through style, not to hide the workings. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/61.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/62.png (every view shows only plain drums). Fix: render the pulley shells that cover the bevel gears (61: the middle and right lower pulleys; 62: the pulleys carrying the second and third bevels) with the 63/70 see-through material, so the three bevels and their meshing show from the default camera.

### 037 — medium
- The spiral of studs on the right cone is a dense bead chain of 20 small studs (spacing about 0.09 of the cone's base width). The plate draws about six large studs across the visible face, spaced about 0.24 of the base width, each about 2.5 times the model's stud diameter. The effect reads as a different mechanism from the plate. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/37.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/37m.png. Fix: in `conical-stud-geometry.js` (studCount 20, studRadius 0.055) reduce the count to about 10–12 over the turn with a stud radius of about 0.1, and re-derive the toothed cone's tooth count and addendum so each stud still takes one tooth. This interacts with the documented two-head silhouette flaw, which a coarser pitch should ease rather than worsen.

### 026 — medium
- The crown wheel's teeth are sharp triangular pyramids, but the plate draws square, flat-topped crown teeth matching the spur's square teeth. The crown body is also a thin disc where the plate draws a deep rim (about 0.1 of its diameter), and the spur gear lacks the hub boss the plate draws through both of its faces (only a thin washer is modelled). Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/26m.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/m2.png (right tile). Fix: generate the crown teeth as rectangular blocks with flat tips and a small flank taper (the same style as 24's square teeth, sized to the spur's pitch), thicken the crown body to the drawn depth, and add a turned hub boss about 1.5 times the shaft diameter spanning both faces of the spur.

### 036 — low
- At the bottom crossover the pinion hangs about half its radius below the mangle wheel's disc edge. The plate keeps the pinion inside a margin of plain disc beyond the outer tooth row. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/36m.png. Fix: enlarge the plain disc radius by about 0.6 of the pinion's tip radius (or pull the track in by the same amount), so the pinion always stays over the disc.

### 039 — low
- The sun and planet gears are the same size (tip diameters about 72 and 75 px in the default view). The plate draws the sun about 18% larger than the planet (about 154 against 130 px). Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/39.png. Fix: if the 2:1 Watt ratio isn't required by the caption (it isn't stated), raise the sun's tooth count by about 20% and lengthen the arm to suit. Otherwise record the deviation in the ledger.

### 047 — low
- The lever's fixed pivot sits on a grey pedestal with a foot plate that Brown doesn't draw. The plate shows only the pivot circle, and the pedestal is prominent in the default view and from behind. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/47.png (0def, 3back). Fix: remove the pedestal and foot. Keep a plain fixed pivot stub, as 48 and 52 do.

### 050, 051 — low
- Both universal joints end in short tapered stubs, about 15% of the view width. The plates draw long, uniform round shafts running to the plate edges (51's run the full width). Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/50.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/51.png. Fix: replace the tapered stubs with straight round shafts of the fork-neck diameter, about three times as long, with plain ends.

### 023 — low
- Weight C is a cylinder, but the plate draws a square block. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/23.png. Fix: make C a rounded-edge box of the same mass footprint, hung from the same eye.

### 028 — low
- The lower disc has a stepped lip round its upper edge (two thicknesses), where the plate draws a plain flat board. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/m2.png (left tile). Fix: make the disc a single cylinder of the drawn thickness.

Screens:

## Findings, 66–75

### 071 — medium
- The two rim slits are cut as tapered swept channels, so the rim ends at each slit in long knife-edge tongues. At phase 0.66 the rim near the upper slit thins to a razor sliver about 0.15 R long, and it looks broken in the rotated view. Brown draws each notch as a plain slot with parallel, square walls. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/71-d.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/71-e.png (also 71-sm.png, sheet 71.png at 5p66). Fix: in `authored-intermittent-core.js` (`channelRegion`, about line 2311), replace the tapering `widthAt` channel with a constant-width straight slot (stud diameter + 0.02) along the chord of the stud's crossing, with square or radiused ends. Then drop any rim remnant thinner than about 0.03 (clip it with a minimum-thickness offset) so no tongue is left. The documented angular offset of the slits can stay.

### 073 — medium
- Fixed spring C is modelled as two straight bars with an angular bend partway up, plus a sharp J-hook at its tip. Brown draws C as one smooth, continuous arc rising from the ground block, with its end simply pointing into A's teeth. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/73-cc.png (sheet 73.png). Fix: build C as one swept strip along a single circular arc (or a two-arc spline) from the block face to the tooth root, with the tip cut to the valley angle. Drop the J-hook and the mid-length kink, and keep the strip thickness constant.

### 069 — low
- Driver B's single tooth is a straight finger with a square, flat end, and the relief beside it is a sharp V-notch. Brown draws a round-ended lobe with a rounded U-notch behind it. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/69-bm.png. Fix: in B's outline, give the tooth tip a full semicircular end (radius = half the finger width) and make the notch a circular arc, keeping the working flank that drives A unchanged.

### 075 — low (documented, but fixable)
- After each drive stroke the wheel still slips back about half a tooth before the click seats. At phase 0.25 the click tip hangs clear of any root. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/75-strip.png (bottom row). Fix: don't reshape the click. Instead, lengthen bar D's drive stroke (or time pawl B's release) so the wheel overtravels just past the click's drop point before B lets go. The click then falls into the root during the drive, and the slip-back shrinks to the small seating backlash the rules allow.

### 066, 067 — low
- The worm threads lean the same way as the plate, but much less steeply: about 10° off the axis normal, against about 25° in the plate, whose worms read as steep, coarse multi-start threads. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/66-wm.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/67-wm.png. Fix: raise the worm lead (two starts, or a larger lead angle) to match the plate's thread slant, and regenerate the 26-tooth wheel's helix angle to suit. This is optional, because it changes the reduction timing.

Screens:
- The disconnected-parts screen flags two things in this range. In 72, the cam B and shaft group floats: it has no bearing, and the plate draws none. In 74, gears A and B are near misses on their own free shafts. Neither is a visible defect.
- The 66 weight arm/sleeve near miss (0.035) is visibly seated in the half-cut sleeve (`z/66-am.png`).
- The coincident-faces screen flags no pairs for 66–75.

Clean: 068, 070, 072, 074.

## Findings, 76–85


### 076 — medium
- The undrawn dark stop heel of dog B, with its grey pin, hangs below the tappet bar in most of the cycle, including the default phase. The ledger says it "shows briefly on the return", but it is visible in about 12 of 14 sampled phases. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/76-strip.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/76-c.png (right tile), /dev/shm/p90/a/z/76-zm.png (middle tile). Fix: put the heel and its slot inside the tappet bar's outline, behind the bar's face, so the stop acts within the bar's depth. Or cut the heel so it never drops below the bar's lower edge. Correct the ledger wording either way.
- An undrawn dark-grey support arm runs from the common axle up to the click's pivot. It shows in the gap between the click and the teeth in the default view, and as a full bar in the back view. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/76-c.png (left tile), /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/76.png (back tile). Fix: hide this bracket in presentation, as 83 does for its physics frame, and leave the click's pivot as a plain fixed stub. If a bracket must stay, shorten it to a small boss behind the click's pivot eye.

### 077 — medium
- Each wheel peg's end face is ringed by a deliberate black ink annulus (`pegRimGeometry`/`pegRimMaterial` = `PALETTE.ink` in `src/simulation/alternating-peg-pawl.js`, about lines 26–33). That is the black outline-rim style the user has banned (261, 272, 276), and it reads as a black rim in the rotated views. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/77-c.png. Fix: delete the rim mesh. Keep the pale cap on the muted peg body, or give the cap a slight chamfer so the edge reads through shading.
- Both hook heads are thin C-shapes about the rod's width. Brown's heads are clearly bolder than their rods. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/77-c.png. Fix (low priority): thicken the C rim to about 1.5× the rod width, with the socket unchanged.

### 080 — low
- The crossed hook pawls' curled ends have uneven thickness. Each thickens at the bend and then tapers to a thin beak, which reads as hand-drawn wobble rather than one constant-width curl. While lifted, the returning hook's tip lies over the tooth face in front of the rack. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/80-z.png. Fix: rebuild each hook as one constant-width arc curl (two concentric circular arcs plus a round tip), identical on both pawls, with the tip angle cut to the tooth valley.

### 082 — medium
- The rotated views show undrawn helical torsion springs and long spacer pins at both pawl pivots, stacked between the red arms and the pawls behind them. The ledger admits the springs are not Brown's, but their visibility is not listed as a flaw. There is also a dark spring leg or tick on each pawl face near its pivot, visible even in the default view. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/82-o.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/82-q.png. Fix: keep the spring torque in the physics but do not render the coils or tick. Seat each pawl directly behind its arm eye on a short pin no longer than the combined thickness of the two plates.

### 083 — medium
- An undrawn grey stirrup-and-coil-spring cage hangs from shaft B at each sector hub. It is clearly visible in both ±50° views, alongside the physics frame that presentation already hides. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/83-c.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/sheets/83.png. Fix: hide these spring cages in presentation, as is done for the rod-A guide and frame. If a return spring is needed visually, it is not drawn, so it must not be rendered.

### 084 — low
- D's arms are straight radial spokes ending in hooked tips. The plate draws D as a cam whose arms are curved, S-flared shapes. The model also has a small undrawn nub, `singleWorkingCam`, on the hub that engages nothing visible. Capture: /dev/shm/p90/a/z/84-c.png, /dev/shm/p90/a/z/84-79.png. Fix: sweep the arms as smooth circular-arc S-curves into the rim, as drawn. Remove the hub nub, or merge it into the hub's outline, if it has no working contact.
- Screen lead: the disconnected-parts screen reports the two end guide posts, each with its foot, as floating, 1.74 from the cam stand's sill. They stand freely with feet in mid-air (no ground plane), but the plate draws them as fixed guides. At most a nitpick: leave them, or trim the feet.
