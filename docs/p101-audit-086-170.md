# Pass 101 audit, lane 086–170

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, pass-101 audit lane (086–170). Date: 2026-09-28. This is a read-only audit of HEAD `253e748`.

All captures and screens are in `/dev/shm/p101/a/`, outside Git. Paths below are relative to that directory.

- **Tiles:** `tiles/NNN.png` holds, in a 3×3 grid, the plate, the default view, +50° yaw/+20° pitch, −50°/−15°, behind, left (90°), right (−90°), top (+75° pitch) and below (−60° pitch).
- **Cycle strips:** `tiles/NNN-cycle.png` holds 12 phases (0, 1/12, …, 11/12) at the default view.
- **Raw views:** `NNN/`.
- **Zooms:** `z/`.
- **Screens:** `disc.json` and `disc2.json` (disconnected parts), `cf.json` (coincident faces), `white.json`, `edge.json` and `facet.json`, all over 86–170.
- **Helpers:**
  - `shots.mjs` and `std.sh` make the captures.
  - `bbox.mjs ID PHASE [regex]` prints world bounds and colour per mesh.
  - `probe.mjs` prints part rotations per phase.

Every movement in the range was viewed in all 8 views plus its 12-phase strip, beside the plate. Suspect spots were zoomed or measured. Each ledger row (`visibleFlaws`/`limits`) and `docs/p96-audit-086-170.md` were read, and the p97–p100 docs were checked for the IDs they touched. Documented forced limits are not re-reported (86's hover, 88's spiral offset, 146's loop width, 153's boss step, 159's slack cord, 127's feet, 132's standalone bed). Neither is the pending "ink shafts" palette question.

## Screen results

- **Disconnected parts:**
  - No floating moving parts.
  - Four detachments are structural or documented, and none is visible:
    - 132's bed stands on the common floor level with no floor drawn (documented).
    - 88's is the bare cam group.
    - 127's are the pump-barrel feet (documented).
    - 129 and 131 show 0.019–0.02 bore/guide clearances.
  - Lips:
    - Reported below: 152 (0.094, groove wall), 161 (0.097, lower support; 0.05, spindle eye) and 151 (0.075, plain shaft over screw core).
    - Small or hidden: 109 (0.022), 124 (0.034, bit shank), 140 (0.022 and 0.015) and 143 (0.013 key).
  - Sliver: 147's crosshead–collar hinge (small patch). The zoom shows it seated, so it is not reported.
- **Coincident faces:** only 87 has a visible pair. `rearInputShaft`'s end face at z −1.05 is coplanar with both `inputHiddenSpoke±1` front faces (area 0.0015 each, contrast 0.52). The other pairs are ≤ 1.7e-5 in area: 115, 116 and 120–125.
- **White parts:** only 152's parchment board (#e9e1cf), which is intended.
- **Edge mounts:** 87's `reversingStud` (ratio 0.65, reported) and `bellRodPin` (0.86, not visible).
- **Faceting:** the highest scores were zoomed.
  - 128's frame outline is visibly segmented (reported).
  - 132's columns, 141's blade, 104's wheel, 110's arms, 114's frame and 147's crosshead are not visibly faceted at normal zoom.

**Correction during the audit:** a first pixel count suggested that 148's pinion and gear have mismatched pitch. The source shows one module (48 teeth at r 4.08 and 12 teeth at r 1.02, centre distance 5.10, 4:1 ratio), and a zoom (`z/148.png`) shows a clean mesh. So 148 is clean.

## Findings (ordered by ID)

### 87 (low)
- **Flaw 1:** the yellow reversing stud on wheel E still spans the band's inner edge, so its boss overhangs the spoke window (edge ratio 0.65; open since p93).
- **Flaw 2:** the rear input shaft's end face is coplanar with the two hidden spokes' faces (flicker screen, contrast 0.52).
- **Flaw 3:** a pale thin bar (the feather key) shows between clutch D and gear C in rotated views.
- **Capture:** `z/87.png` (panel 1), `z/87w.png` (right), `cf.json`.
- **Fix:**
  - Move the stud to the band's mid-radius, or shrink its boss to about 0.8× the band width, and re-time the strike on G.
  - End the shaft 0.005 inside the hub.
  - Colour the key steel.

### 90, 91 (low; open since p90)
- **Flaw 1:** the yoke stems are still round rods. Brown draws flat bars filleted into the yoke.
- **Flaw 2:** 91's yoke top and bottom edges slope about 1.2° (5 px over 240 px, in every phase). This is traced wobble on a part that should be level and symmetric.
- **Capture:** `tiles/090.png`, `tiles/091.png`, `tiles/091-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** make the stems a rectangular section in the yoke extrusion, with fillets. Rebuild 91's frame outline from level straight edges and arcs.

### 92 (low)
- **Flaw:** the connecting rod and the slide block are the same blue. Where the rod's end lies on the block, in every phase, they merge into one blob.
- **Capture:** `tiles/092-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** colour the block grey or brass.

### 99 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the frame rails are still thin wire posts that end free at both ends, reached from the guide block only by brackets behind it.
- **Capture:** `tiles/099.png` (yawm, below).
- **Fix:** thicken the rails to about 0.16 and close the brackets into the block's extrusion.

### 100 (low)
- **Flaw:** the orange disk turns with the crank (the probe shows it rotating 1.94 → 0.37 rad), but it carries no quadrant speed cue. The only bands on it are the lever's shadow.
- **Capture:** `z/v.png` (panel 1), `tiles/100-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** apply the shared odd/even quadrant faces, as 88, 97 and 99 have.

### 102, 103 (low; open since p90)
- **Flaw:** the square threads are still deep, thin fins with lands of about ⅓ of the pitch.
- **Capture:** `tiles/102.png`, `tiles/103.png`.
- **Fix:** use lands of about 0.5 × pitch and a depth of about 0.5 × pitch.

### 107 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the serpentine groove shows about 5 turn-arounds on the visible face, against about 8 on the plate.
- **Capture:** `tiles/107.png`.
- **Fix:** use 8–10 strokes per revolution if the follower allows it; otherwise document the count as forced.

### 111 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the thread run-outs still end in thin tapering fins that poke past both ends of the orange sleeve and the blue screw tip.
- **Capture:** `tiles/111-cycle.png` (c00, c04, c06).
- **Fix:** end each thread square, or with a short radial ramp, flush with the end face (in the shared helical-thread helper).

### 117 (low)
- **Flaw:** the yoke is now a busy cage of four rails, eight collars, four nuts, bolt ends and front and rear forks, all in one blue. It hides the cam and rollers in the default view. Brown draws a plain two-rail frame with two crossheads and two roller forks.
- **Capture:** `tiles/117.png`, `z/117.png`.
- **Fix:**
  - Make each side one flat rail plate spanning the depth, rather than front and rear rail pairs with collars and nuts.
  - Keep the two-cheek forks.
  - Colour the rollers' forks or the rails in a contrasting tone.

### 118 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the fixed rack base still has a square, stepped left end against a rounded right end. Brown rounds both.
- **Capture:** `tiles/118-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** mirror the right end's arc onto the left end.

### 123 (low)
- **Flaw:** the centre gear, which carries the curved catch, and the double rack are the same blue and overlap. The stop and curved-piece hand-over that the caption describes can't be read.
- **Capture:** `tiles/123-cycle.png` (c03–c05).
- **Fix:** give the centre gear a contrasting colour, for example brass.

### 125 (low)
- **Flaw:** the floating bars are the same gold as the middle gear. In c04–c10 the lower bar and its rods cross the gold gear and merge with it.
- **Capture:** `tiles/125-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** colour the bars grey or brass.

### 128 (low; open since p93)
- **Flaw 1:** the frame's side lugs are still flat-tipped wedges. Brown draws rounded knobs on a short neck.
- **Flaw 2:** the barrel-shaped frame outline is visibly segmented in rotated views (the faceting screen gives 6 edges up to 21°).
- **Capture:** `tiles/128.png`, `z/fac.png` (panel 3), `facet.json`.
- **Fix:** add a neck and circular-arc knob in the same extrusion, and resample the outline arcs at 2° or finer.

### 131 (medium)
- **Flaw 1:** the slotted sector, its teeth and the rack are all the same blue (#315f78) in the same plane (z 0–0.14). The working mesh reads as one blue mass — the "blue-on-blue" case the user flagged at 132.
- **Flaw 2 (low):** the sector has 9 teeth, against about 11 drawn.
- **Capture:** `z/v.png` (panel 3), `tiles/131-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** colour the rack bar and its teeth grey or gold. Optionally, extend the sector to about 11 teeth.

### 139 (medium)
- **Flaw 1:** the endless internal rack is a decal-thin sheet: `steel-tooth-rim` spans z −0.003…0.003 and `rack-backing` is 0.002 deep. The pinion is 0.114 thick, so in rotated views it stands proud of a paper-thin toothed edge and the teeth mesh with nothing solid.
- **Flaw 2 (low):** the rack teeth are pointed, where Brown draws square teeth, and the pinion has 8 teeth against about 10 drawn.
- **Capture:** `z/139.png` (panel 1 rotated, panel 2 default, panel 3 plate).
- **Fix:** extrude the rack plate (the opening with its teeth) to at least the pinion's face width (about 0.12), with the backing behind it. Use a square stub tooth, as 115 does, and rebake.

### 141 (low)
- **Flaw:** Brown dots the lower band wheel where it passes behind the curved table bracket. The model's opaque bracket and post cut across that wheel.
- **Capture:** `z/v.png` (panel 2), `tiles/141.png`.
- **Fix:** use the shared see-through style on the table bracket.

### 142 (low)
- **Flaw:** the rod and the planet gear are both blue. In c05–c07 the rod crosses the planet and they merge.
- **Capture:** `tiles/142-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** colour the rod grey or brass.

### 143 (low)
- **Flaw:** the worm wheel, its crank arm and the sliding carriage are all the same blue. The crank arm, which drives the gold link, is nearly invisible in c05–c10.
- **Capture:** `z/v.png` (panel 4), `tiles/143-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** give the carriage frame grey, or the crank a contrasting colour.

### 147 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** each fan arm still just runs across its fan plate with no fastening. Brown draws a flat strap bolted with two bolts.
- **Capture:** `tiles/147.png`.
- **Fix:** end each arm in a strap lying on the fan face, with two bolt heads, in one extrusion.

### 150 (low)
- **Flaw:** the rocking lever is now flat, but it is only 0.03 thick (z −0.335…−0.305). Beside the 0.54-wide roller and the 0.17 rod it reads as a paper blade in the rotated and top views.
- **Capture:** `tiles/150.png`, `bbox.mjs 150 0`.
- **Fix:** thicken it to about 0.10 and adjust the roller-axle and rod-pin lengths.

### 151 (low)
- **Flaw:** the plain shaft (section 0.42) steps out 0.075 over the screw core (0.27) where each thread ends, which shows as a collar-like lip beside the wheel hub. Brown's shaft is one diameter.
- **Capture:** `disc2.json`, `z/161.png` (panel 3).
- **Fix:** make the plain shaft the core's diameter, or taper it into the core.

### 152 (low; open since p96)
- **Flaw:** the grooved cross is still built from separate floor, wall, corner and base pieces. The 0.094 lip of the horizontal groove wall over the base persists, and a step shows at the arm end.
- **Capture:** `z/152.png` (panel 2), `disc.json`.
- **Fix:** make the cross one extrusion (the outline minus the two slots) with a single floor.

### 155 (low)
- **Flaw:** the vertical rod and the rocking lever it is pinned to are the same orange (#de5a3f) and overlap at the joint, so the rod reads as part of the lever.
- **Capture:** `tiles/155.png`.
- **Fix:** colour the rod grey or brass.

### 161 (medium)
- **Flaw 1:** the lower support is still the open-slotted fork bar, butted against the side of the round lower bearing boss — a bar perched on a cylinder with no fillet. It overhangs the boss's bottom by 0.097 (screen lip). This is the "disc perched on a block" pattern the user rejected.
- **Flaw 2 (low):** the 0.2-diameter spindle runs into an end eye only 0.1 thick, so the rod stands 0.05 proud of both faces.
- **Capture:** `z/161.png` (panels 1–2), `disc2.json`.
- **Fix:**
  - Make the support one tapered extrusion wrapping the boss, with tangent, filleted joins and the boss's depth.
  - Thicken the eye to the rod's diameter.

### 164 (low; open since p90)
- **Flaw:** the press output is still nearly invisible. The top plank rises about 7 px of 300 over the cycle while the lever swings about 25°.
- **Capture:** `tiles/164-cycle.png`.
- **Fix:** amplify the platen's travel, or show a workpiece compressing. Otherwise record the stroke as a limit.
