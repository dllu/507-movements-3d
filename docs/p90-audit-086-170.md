## Findings

### 98 (medium)
- **Flaw:** the default "section view" hides the arm's front `cover` (`mujoco-endless-groove/geometry.js`, `setSectionView(true)` sets `parts.cover.visible=false`). With the cover gone, the groove island `inner` has nothing visibly joining it to the outer arm, so it floats. This is the 297 pattern the user rejected.
- **Capture:** `z/m98h.png` (disk hidden, from behind), `z/m98.png`.
- **Fix:** keep the cover and give it the shared semi-transparent see-through style, as for 63, 70 and 71, instead of removing it. The pin and groove then read through a connected arm.

### 115 (medium)
- **Flaw:** both pinions and both racks have narrow, sharply pointed star-like teeth. Brown draws square, flat-topped teeth with flat roots. This is the tooth-proportion rule from 388.
- **Capture:** `z/115-g.png`, `tiles/115.png`.
- **Fix:** regenerate with stub or short-addendum teeth: a flat top land of about 0.35 × pitch, as 114 and 116 already use (14.5°–20°, addendum ≈ 0.8 m). Re-check the pinion-on-rack handover in the bake.

### 121 (medium)
- **Flaw:** at phases 0 and 0.67 the click's nose rests at tooth-tip height against a cog flank, not down in a root. Brown draws the nose seated between two teeth, and the user requires pawls and clicks to nestle fully in the root.
- **Capture:** `z/m121.png` (panels 1 and 3; panel 2, at 0.33, is seated).
- **Fix:** add the small cog backlash or phase offset that lets the click drop fully into the root before each index (as 116 did in p86). Shape the nose tip to the valley angle.

### 133 (medium)
- **Flaw:** the crank pinion has 8 teeth (`authored-gears-core.js`, `handCrankPinionSectorRodPress`: `pinionTeeth = 8`). Brown draws about 12, and the modelled teeth are thin spikes. The pinion reads as a star.
- **Capture:** `z/m133b.png` (plate crop beside the model), `tiles/133.png`.
- **Fix:** use 12 teeth with a sector pitch to match (sector equivalent 72, or keep ratio 6 with installed teeth rescaled), and give them Brown's square proportions.

### 148 (medium)
- **Flaw:** the gear's eight spokes and the two groove walls are wire-thin rods. In Brown's drawing the spokes are broad double-line arms and the groove is a solid band. At normal zoom the wheel reads as a flimsy wire frame.
- **Capture:** `z/m148.png`, `tiles/148.png`.
- **Fix:** make the wheel one 2D extrusion using the shared four-spoke/rim builder's approach, with spokes about 2.5× wider. Make the groove one extruded band with a channel cut, not two thin walls. Keep the reduced outer lobe that the ledger documents.

### 157 (medium)
- **Flaw:** the bell crank swings about 100°. Its output arm turns from horizontal to pointing straight down, so the "rectilinear" rod tilts 15–20° and its lower end sweeps sideways. Brown draws a modest swing about the drawn pose, with the rod vertical.
- **Capture:** `z/m157.png` (phases 0, 0.55, 0.6, 0.7 and 0.8), `tiles/157.png`.
- **Fix:** reduce the crank radius, as 158 did, or lengthen the input arm so the crank stays within about ±30° of the drawn pose. Alternatively, ideally guide the rod's lower end on the vertical line as in 156, and state the residual.

### 168 and 169 (medium)
- **Flaw:** in both movements the rocker lever runs to an inferred remote pivot about 4 mechanism-lengths away. In 168 it goes straight up to a small grey bracket. In 169 it runs off the bottom-left of the default frame.
- **Effect:** the working linkage is small in the default view, and in 169 the rocker is cropped by the frame. Brown breaks the rocker off just past the pitman joint.
- **Capture:** `z/m168.png` (both at 2.2× zoom-out), `tiles/168.png`, `tiles/169.png`.
- **Fix:** move the inferred rocker pivot much closer, about one pitman-length, with a minimal pivot as in 145. Or end the rocker at Brown's break and guide its end ideally, as in 156 and 166. Then refit the camera to the linkage.

### Low findings
- **90, 91:**
  - **Flaw:** the yoke stems are plain round rods. Brown draws flat bars that flare into the yoke.
  - **Capture:** `z/m92.png` (panel 3), `tiles/90.png`.
  - **Fix:** use rectangular stems blended into the yoke ring.
- **92:**
  - **Flaw:** from behind, an undrawn grey support arm runs from the guide's end back to the wheel hub. The screen also flags a 0.0085 lip at `guide`/`rearSupport`.
  - **Capture:** `z/m92.png` (panel 2).
  - **Fix:** hide it (the ledger already says undrawn guide hardware is not shown) or shorten it out of view.
- **102, 111:**
  - **Flaw:** the square threads are thin, deep fins with lands about ¼ pitch. Brown's square thread has lands of about ½ pitch. 102's bolt tip also ends flush in the nut.
  - **Capture:** `z/m102b.png`, `z/m111.png`.
  - **Fix:** use a square-thread profile with a 50% land.
- **109:**
  - **Flaw:** the carriage notch around the guide is slightly ragged, with a lip where `arm` meets `carriage` (0.023).
  - **Capture:** `z/m109l.png`.
  - **Fix:** seat the arm fully in the carriage boss.
- **116:**
  - **Flaw:** the front pawl is a thin grey sliver of a hook. It is flimsy, although it does seat.
  - **Capture:** `z/m116.png`.
  - **Fix:** thicken it to a smooth two-arc plate like the rear pawl.
- **118:**
  - **Flaw:** the fixed rack's web shows rectangular windows with pill-shaped solid bosses inside. Brown's `( )` marks read as plain oval openings or recesses in a solid web.
  - **Capture:** `z/m118.png`.
  - **Fix:** use one extrusion with oval holes.
- **122:**
  - **Flaw:** the vertical connecting link is lens-shaped: wider in the middle, with a bowed edge. Brown draws a straight link of uniform width.
  - **Capture:** `z/m122.png`.
  - **Fix:** a straight bar with circular-arc ends concentric with the pins.
- **127, 128, 129:**
  - **Flaw:** some members are thinner than drawn: 127's racks are narrower than the drawn bars, 128's wiper arms are thin, and 129's frame posts are sticks where Brown draws plank uprights.
  - **Fix:** thicken them to the plate's proportions.
- **132:**
  - **Flaw:** the anvil block's base sits below the column feet, so the two don't share one ground level. It touches nothing (screen: floating, gap 1.25).
  - **Capture:** `z/m132lv.png`.
  - **Fix:** put the block and the feet on one ground level.
- **134:**
  - **Flaw:** Brown's segmented, hatched lagging rim and large hub ring are omitted. The wheel is a plain four-spoke pulley.
  - **Fix:** add the rim segments as one extrusion.
- **146:**
  - **Flaw:** the yoke is a rounded rectangle. Brown's is a horseshoe or shield-shaped U.
  - **Fix:** retrace the yoke with arcs.
- **147:**
  - **Flaw:** the bulb's top finial knob is omitted, and the dish's underside is plain.
  - **Capture:** `z/m147x.png`.
- **152:**
  - **Flaw:** an undrawn grey rectangular board stands behind the ellipse. Brown draws only the elliptical outline.
  - **Fix:** use an elliptical board, or drop the board and keep the traced ellipse.
- **153:**
  - **Flaw:** the elbow lever's arms are thin sticks with a flat-cut lower end. Brown draws broad arms with rounded ends.
  - **Fix:** one extrusion with arc ends.
- **164:**
  - **Flaw:** the press stroke is about 1% of the view height, so the upper beam barely moves.
  - **Capture:** `z/m164.png`.
  - **Note:** this is realistic near dead centre. Consider a slightly larger lever sweep.
