## Cross-cutting root cause (426, 427, 429; also 418 and 422–425)

`applySourcePresentation` → `creaseNormalsIn` → `smoothFacetNormals`, in `src/simulation/crease-normals.js` (added in 10a65f3), runs once at load on every non-indexed BufferGeometry. That includes each `steamVolume` from `steam-section-kit.js`.

The function ends with `geometry.setAttribute('normal', new BufferAttribute(...))`. But `steamVolume.setRegion` still writes each frame's normals into its own closed-over array, which is no longer attached to the geometry. From the second frame on, the cap triangles carry side-wall normals.

Evidence:
- At 426 phase 0.66, 112 cap triangles in the z = −0.015 plane have horizontal normals.
- A direct factory call in Node, without the presentation pass, gives correct normals.
- `/dev/shm/p93/f/f1-dyn.mjs` lists every mesh whose position attribute is DynamicDraw but whose normal attribute is not. It finds them in 418, 422–429. In 428 the affected meshes are static channels, so nothing shows.

### 484 — high
- **Handedness.** The helix is mirrored:
  - Brown's front crossing of the blade over the drum is "/", running from the upper right-hand lobe down-left, and his dashed (hidden) crossing is "\" on the left.
  - Ours crosses the front "\", running from the left-hand lobe down-right.
  - A helix turning in place never changes the sense of its crossing, so this is a mirror, not a phase difference.
- **Proportions (secondary).** The lobes rise about 0.6 drum diameters above the drum; Brown's rise about 0.85. The standards are thin square posts where Brown draws wide plank standards.
- Captures: `z/484m.png` (plate beside default), `sheets/484.png`.
- Fix: flip the sign of the helix sweep in the 484 factory and re-phase so the two upper lobes and the lower lobe fall where drawn. Raise the blade's outer radius, and use plank standards.
