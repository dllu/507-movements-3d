Scratch files are in `/dev/shm/p90/f/`, outside Git.
- **Tiles:** `cap/ID/tile.png` for every ID. Each tile shows the plate, the default view, yaw +50°/pitch 20°, yaw −50°/pitch −15°, the back view, phase 0.33 and phase 0.66. The single views are in the same directory.

  - `cf.json` and `cf-summary.txt` come from `screen-coincident-faces.mjs --jobs=8`, filtered to contrast ≥ 0.1 and area ≥ 1e-4 × diag².

## Per movement

### 462 — high
- **Floating flume.** The receiving flume under the spout lip floats in mid-air with no support. The disconnected-parts screen marks `receiving-flume-under-spout-lip` floating, 0.585 (0.084 × diag) from anything. Brown shows the delivery landing on the bank at the left.
  - Capture: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/462a.png`, `cap/462/tile.png`.
  - Fix: build that bank as a solid block from the pool edge up to the lip level, and rest the flume on it, or let the bank's top be the flume.
- **Wheel shape.** Both chain wheels are heavy notched rings with small pegs. Brown draws open stars: 8 long radial arms round a small hub, with the chain on the arm tips.
  - Capture: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/462b.png` beside the plate.
  - Fix: rebuild each wheel as a hub with 8 radial arm extrusions, and seat the chain links at the arm tips.
- **Speckle.** The upper wheel shows speckled hatching where the black spokes meet the ring's inner face, which looks like coplanar faces.
  - Capture: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/462b.png`, `462d.png`.
  - Fix: end the spokes 0.005 inside the ring.

### 468 — high
- The plate's elevation and plan are built as two separate models (`plate-elevation-*` and `plate-plan-*`), so every view shows two joints. The user's standing rule is that a plate drawing one mechanism twice gets ONE animated model (262/263, 294/295, 387).
- Capture: `cap/468/tile.png`.
- Fix: keep one joint with its timber frames and animate the ball's deflection through both the elevation bend and the plan bend; the viewer can rotate to the plan. Drop the `plate-plan-*` parts. That also removes one copy of the collar/ball coincident-face pair, which is 1e-4 × diag² at contrast 0.12.

### 445 and 446 (one shared model) — medium
- An undrawn, wide, half-cut cylinder spans the open air gap between the upper box's orifice and the lower box's top opening. The free stream (445) and the checked cone and rising column (446), which Brown draws in that gap, are hidden inside a pipe that isn't there.
  - The side view and the back view show the cylinder plainly.
  - Sub-lane f1 rated 445 clean; the lane agent found this on 446, and the two use the same model.
- Captures: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/446a.png`, `446b.png`, `446c.png` (side view), and the plate `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/p445.png`.
- Fix: in `authored-oscillating-water-columns.js`, drop the connecting cylinder and keep the drawn upper orifice and the lower box's turned-down lip. The revolved water body then shows in open air.

### 460 — medium
- **Invisible discharge.** The scoop fills and drains (it is empty by phase 0.5), but its discharge into the left channel is only a nearly invisible needle at the lip, around phases 0.42–0.46. The lifted water seems to vanish.
  - Captures: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/m11.png`, `m12.png`.
  - Fix: emit a sheet stream as wide as the scoop mouth from the lip into the channel, with its flow tied to the scoop's drain schedule.
- **Lever shape.** The top lever is a thin bar with 2–3 notches. Brown draws a deep, tapered lattice beam with a row of about 6 teeth at its free end.
  - Capture: `cap/460/tile.png`.
  - Fix: rebuild the lever as that beam, with the tooth row, as one extrusion.

### 471 — medium

- Captures: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/471a.png`, `471b.png`, `471c.png`, `cap/471/d_back.png`.
- Fix: under the see-through rule, make the occluding frame cheek (and B's skirt, if it covers the crank) semi-transparent in the shared style. Alternatively, move the crank disc and D into the drawn plane in front of B's guides.

### 475 — medium
- **Wall port.** Where steam pipe A passes through the wall of chamber D, the section leaves a thin wedge sliver of wall over the pipe and a square-cornered notch round it. It is not a round port fitted to the pipe, and it reads as a leak path.
  - Captures: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/475d.png`, `475e.png`, `475a.png`.
  - Fix: bore the wall with a round hole of the pipe's radius plus 0.002 on the pipe axis, as 476's sealed port does, and cap the section across it.
- **Shadow acne.** The inside of chamber D shows blocky, checkerboard shading on the curved shell, plainly visible in the default view.
  - Captures: `/dev/shm/p90/f/z/475a.png`, `475b.png`.
  - Fix: raise the shadow normalBias for this shell, or turn off receiveShadow on its inner face.
- **Flange (low).** Brown's flange on the outer end of A is missing.
  - Fix: add a short flange ring.

### 429 — medium (sub-lane f1)

### 495 — medium (sub-lane f4)
