# Pass-51 lane m10-48-147: minor residuals in 48, 55, 108, 118, 137, 147

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5 (lane m10-48-147), 2026-09-24. I captured each ID
with `scripts/review-movement-source-views.mjs` from a private non-watching dev
server, before and after each change, and checked several motion phases of
each changed movement. Earlier work on these IDs is in
[m3-mujoco-cams-review.md](m3-mujoco-cams-review.md).

## 48 — jaw clutch: rounded troughs

- **Change:** each trough between the jaw crests is now a concave half-ellipse,
  0.06 deep at the flanks and flat only at its centre. The profile is now a
  continuous wave with rounded crests and rounded troughs, as Brown draws it.
  The axial driving bands are unchanged: they span the trough edge (0.06) to
  the crest base (0.15), and they still overlap 0.06 when engaged.
  `troughDepth` is in `jaw-clutch-motion.js`.
- **Depth limit:** a one-dimensional sweep over the motion cycle (2,001 poses ×
  4,096 angles) finds 0.0014 of clearance at 0.06, 0.0003 at 0.065 and
  overlap at 0.07. The mating crest's shoulder sits over the trough near the
  driving flank.
- **Shared generator:** a raised trough meets a vertical flank above the rim,
  which would leave a T-junction in the side walls. `jawClutchGeometry` now
  splits the taller side-wall edge at the flank's lower height. This only
  happens when both ends of the jump are above zero, so the other callers
  (reversing clutch, weighted clutch) build the same geometry as before. Their
  16 tests pass.
- **Tests:** all 8 tests in `tests/jaw-clutch.test.mjs` pass: closed solids,
  no jaw penetration over 512 poses, and flank contact error 1.6e-8. The
  `models.test` block passes.
- **Screen (0.01 spacing, 129 poses):** coaxial 0.0020 (pins in bores), solid
  0.0000 (jaw contact). Same as before.

## 55 — coaxial gears: squarer, flat-rooted teeth

- **Change:** A and B are stub teeth (addendum 0.75, dedendum 0.95). A small
  cutter tip radius (0.012) gives them flat roots with small fillets instead of
  round-bottomed gaps. Ring C is shallower (addendum 0.07, dedendum 0.095).
  The teeth now read as square-ish blocks on flat roots.
- **Limit:** the flanks are still 25° involutes, so the teeth taper slightly.
  A 10-tooth pinion needs about 25° to avoid undercut. A lower angle would need
  profile shifts on all three gears, and the internal-gear helper does not
  support shifts. With addendum 0.75, the A/B contact ratio is about 1.04.
  At 0.8 the polygon contact-normal check fails.
- **Tests:** all 4 tests in `tests/coaxial-gears.test.mjs` pass. Working
  contact gaps stay within 1.5e-5 to 5e-5 at all 260 phases, with
  compressive torque. The `models.test` block passes.
- **Screen:** no pairs.

## 108 — reverse-thread barrel: double-start groove (live)

- **Finding:** the plate's crossings sit at the front centre and also at both
  silhouette edges, where the grooves meet midway between the front X's. That
  is a double-start pattern: two starts per hand. The measured 42.8 px
  crossing pitch is then half the lead, and the 85.7 px lead gives Brown's
  steep, nearly straight slopes (about 24° average from centre to edge).
  The earlier conflict came from reading the pattern as single-start.
- **Change:** `profile.js` takes `starts` (default 2). The lead is doubled, and
  each traverse is 2.5 barrel turns. The groove set is the union of the path
  and its copy rotated by π. For two starts the phase is π/2. The reversals
  and same-start crossings then fall at the silhouette edges, and the five
  front crossings stay at the source positions.
- **Physics** (tuned by a forked sub-agent, checked here):
  - `shoeLength` 0.36 → 0.25. The shorter shoe still bridges the 38°
    crossings.
  - `reversalAngle` 3 → 3.5.
  - `coreRadius` 0.4338 → 0.42. The tilted shoe's smallest radius is 0.4259.
  - Default `period` 20 → 30 s. The barrel turns at 1.05 rad/s.
- **Tests:** all 4 tests in `tests/mujoco-reverse-thread-candidate.test.mjs`
  pass with unchanged thresholds. Travel error is 0.882 px, penetration
  0.0615 px, input error 1.2e-5 rad, and p95 speed error 1.73%.
  - Two poses in the tests changed. The deflected-shoe core check now tilts
    by `initialTilt + 0.3`, since the nominal tilt is 0.33.
  - The passive-output check now runs `update(3)` so the slower barrel can
    turn 3 rad.
- **Long run:** a six-cycle (180 s) `probe-reverse-thread-dynamics.mjs` run
  gives:
  - travel error 0.882 px
  - penetration 0.0615 px (`land633`/`shoe7` at 172.46 s)
  - maximum 100 ms speed error 4.6%
  - input error 1.2e-5 rad

  The old configuration's documented long-run peak was 0.367 px. Penetration
  is sensitive to nearby settings: 0.13–0.26 px at period 28/34, shoe length
  0.24/0.27 or reversal angle 3.4/3.6.
- **Visual:** the default capture shows five X crossings with diamonds
  between them and half-diamond triangles at both edges, as on the plate.
  - During rotation the pattern shears helically, as a real barrel does.
  - The grooves stop about 20 px (plate scale) short of the barrel ends, where
    Brown carries partial diamonds to the rims.
  - `source.js`'s qualification and the reconstruction note are updated.

## 118 — stroke doubler: tighter swept framing (live)

- **Crop question:** Brown draws both racks whole, with finished ends, and
  only the pitman as a broken end. A crop matching broken rack ends would
  therefore not be legitimate.
- **Change:** the pitman stroke amplitude (an inferred input, not drawn) is
  0.5, down from 0.9. The upper rack now travels ±1.0 instead of ±1.8. The
  pinion still turns ±61°, and the upper rack's doubled travel is plain. The
  camera still fits the whole swept silhouette, which is now narrower. The
  racks fill 560 of 850 px at rest, up from 455; maxNdc is 0.889.
- **Tests:** all 6 tests in `tests/mujoco-stroke-doubler.test.mjs` pass. The
  stroke thresholds now derive from `profile.amplitude` rather than a
  hard-coded 0.9 stroke. The `models.test` block passes.
- The registry intersection screen loads the legacy authored 118, not this
  production MuJoCo model. Its pairs do not describe production. The native
  tooth-contact tests are the evidence.

## 137 — expansion eccentric: no change

The fork's two rollers stay a fixed distance apart, so every point of the cam
outline passes under a roller in each turn: no part of the edge is free to
carry decoration. Brown's dimples are narrower than the roller radius, so no
roller can follow them. The m3 conjugate fit (3.93 px RMS to the plate's
landmarks) remains the best honest profile. `authored-cams.js` was not
touched, so 138 needs no refresh.

## 147 — fan governor: display phase shows the symmetric dish

- **Finding:** the two-fold ramp dish reads as Brown's symmetric concave arc
  when both ramp crests stand at the silhouette edges. At that moment a roller
  sits at each upper corner, as on the plate. The recorded loop started about
  0.8 rad of shaft angle earlier, when a crest stood in front of the right
  roller.
- **Change:** `baked/fan-governor.js` shows the periodic loop from 0.26 s
  after its recorded start (`displayTimeOffset`). The bake, physics and
  geometry are unchanged, so no rebake and no validation regeneration were
  needed. The loop wraps, and the seam and interpolation tests pass.
- **Limit:** each roller sits partway up its ramp, about 0.5 rad behind the
  crest. At the symmetric-dish pose the crosshead is therefore turned about
  0.5 rad, and the fans are slightly turned rather than face-on.
  - The right roller is partly behind its crest.
  - As the dish turns, its rim shows the two ramps unevenly again.
  - A dish that stays symmetric all the way round would need ramps that end
    at the rollers' working crest: new track geometry and a new bake.
- **Tests:** all tests in `tests/fan-governor-baked.test.mjs` and
  `tests/fan-governor-output.test.mjs` pass, as does the `models.test`
  block.
