# Gas meter and regulator family: 481–483

Primary sources: [481 wet gas meter](https://507movements.com/mm_481.html), [482 Powers gas regulator](https://507movements.com/mm_482.html), and [483 dry gas meter](https://507movements.com/mm_483.html). The official pages mark all three animations unavailable and contain no animation model. Captions and engravings establish the architecture and direction of operation; reconstructed dimensions and prescribed timing remain explicit assumptions.

## Bounded working corrections

`gas-meter-working-parts.js` reuses finite pipe walls, bored turned sections, polygon unions and slotted plates. All three retain their existing analytical schedules and metering/pressure equations. Eight-second playback is preserved through display timing. Ground and fog are removed; source-facing full-cycle bounds replace the oblique default framing.

**481:** The fixed inlet previously shared a solid axle and bent into the rotating partitions. It now passes through real hollow end journals and turns upward within the central clearance. Its complete outlet bore is above the displayed water surface. Finite drum heads have actual central inlet and peripheral outlet apertures, replacing colored solid blocks; partitions meet those heads. Finite case/drum walls and a bored rear case head complete the selected structural interfaces. Lower water and head opacity keeps the chamber geometry readable.

**482:** Mercury troughs have open finite cavities and floors below the moving skirts. The broad cup has front and rear skirts as well as its original side skirts, dipping into one continuous rectangular channel. The smaller valve retains its source-informed notches, now extruded into finite walls; painted triangles no longer cover the openings. The valve axis was shifted slightly inward to clear the surrounding cup trough. Real bores replace the solid inlet/outlet and cup-rod guide. The fixed roof and side wall have corresponding openings. Finite horizontal pin slots replace the varying-length nominal short links: they accommodate the small horizontal excursion of a rigid reversing lever while the cup and valve retain their vertical laws. The lever has a bored fulcrum and connected fixed stand. The unsupported fixed valve-guide ornament is hidden; a shorter valve stem remains. Front cutaway and fluid opacity expose the working mechanism.

**483:** The three-port seat is bored, and the slide valve has finite skirts resting on that seat rather than penetrating it. Its hollow hood covers the central exhaust and selected chamber port at each completed switch. Actual inlet, outlet and branch passages replace tube skins; the fixed bellows end plates and gallery provide their openings. The moving crosshead and flag rods pass through a real gallery slot. The valve stem guides are slotted; a bored rocker pin drives a vertical lost-motion slot on the stem, replacing the changing-length connector. Its fulcrum is supported behind the valve chest. The chest is a finite open interior, with roof/rear openings for the inlet and moving stem assembly. The prescribed stroke/dwell and register-count laws are retained.

## Residuals

The wet drum is still driven by its prescribed angular law; chamber filling is a nominal phase/capacity model, not a free-surface/contact solution. Its sector gas envelopes and common water envelope are illustrative and are not Boolean-subtracted from one another or from partitions. Port sealing and pressure-driven drum torque have not been qualified. The regulator retains its disclosed quasi-static pressure balance and mercury-head assumptions; inertia, friction, capillarity, leakage and seal transients are not integrated, and mercury envelopes include immersed walls. The dry meter retains its prescribed smooth bellows strokes, valve dwell and illustrative over-center spring; it is not a passive spring/pressure simulation. Its flexible bellows and fluid marker routes are not a complete gas-tight, contact-qualified manifold. These selected geometry corrections do not claim exhaustive all-pairs collision freedom. No new MuJoCo study was needed to preserve the known analytic laws within this bounded scope.
