# Water-sealed air pump: 473

The [official source](https://507movements.com/mm_473.html) describes an inverted
bell, water seal, shaft inlet and two upward-opening checks. The page explicitly
marks animation unavailable (checked September 15, 2026). The engraving shows
crossed overhead levers and suspension ropes, a tall bell, and a water line below
the outer tub's rim.

The old short inner lever arms never crossed the center; the short bell and high
water line also differed from that arrangement. Longer inner arms, a 2.18-unit
bell, a lower external water line and a smaller angular stroke now reproduce the
layout. Both suspension ropes retain exact 1.5-unit lengths, with the bell rim
submerged throughout the cycle. Dimensions remain reconstruction choices, not
measurements supplied by Brown.

Shared finite turned walls and plates provide full transparent tub/bell walls,
bored roof, inlet/outlet tubes, check seats and floor penetrations. The support
frame sits behind the moving lever planes. Both levers, their frame and bearing
collars have actual fulcrum bores. The water display is partitioned around the
bell wall and central inlet instead of extending through those solids.

The pressure model remains an analytic polytropic sealed charge with hydrostatic
head and ideal check thresholds. Visible check lift now eases continuously after
each threshold and seats at stroke reversal. This removes the old instantaneous
0.1-unit jumps; it is an illustrative lift law, not an integrated valve-force
solution. The actual upper disk now rests on its finite seat instead of entering
it by 0.0035 units.

## Validation and limits

Eleven existing tests retain exact rope lengths, analytic velocities and
accelerations, pressure/head laws, submerged seal margins and cycle closure.
Three added checks sample selected rendered working surfaces at 33 poses, check
valve-lift continuity at 10,001 phases and verify stable playback buffers. The
surface checks include moving levers/frame journals, bell/tub/inlet clearances,
water/wall separation, floor/roof bores and both valve seats. Sampling is not an
exhaustive collision proof.

The four-second authored period is enforced through production playback. Ground
and material fog are disabled; source-facing full-cycle bounds include the
operating levers and ropes. Source/default/oblique browser inspection found no page errors or clipping;
the maximum absolute projected vertex coordinate was 0.874 over 17 cycle poses.
Transparent vessel/fluid surfaces no longer receive opaque shadows that obscure
the internal pipework.

External water level remains prescribed: finite-tub water conservation and water
inertia are not solved. Gas volumes and plumes are schematic, including around
the valves. Bell mass, operator work, slack rope response, pressure losses and
check inertia remain unvalidated. The ideal threshold state can indicate a check
open while the illustrative disk is still lifting or seating; no finite-valve
flow resistance is inferred from that animation.
