# Pass-51 minor-residuals lane m5 (326–400)

This lane covers the twenty movements the ledger rated **minor**: 326, 327, 328, 333, 340, 345, 348, 351, 354, 358, 364, 365, 371, 372, 376, 377, 381, 384, 389 and 400.

For each ID, captures were taken with `scripts/review-movement-source-views.mjs` from a private non-watching Vite server, before and after the edits, at several phases. Intersections were screened with `scripts/show-body-intersections.mjs` at 0.01 spacing over 129 poses. The captures live in `/dev/shm/m5/{a,b,c,d}` and are not in Git.
