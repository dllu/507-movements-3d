# Pass 101 audit, movements 426–507

Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, pass-101 read-only audit lane. Date: 2026-09-28. Nothing under `src/`, `tests/` or `docs/` changed except this file.

## Method

Scratch files are in `/dev/shm/p101/r426/`, outside Git. The capture paths below are relative to that folder.

- **Contact sheets.** `sheets/ID.png` is a 3×3 grid:
  - Top row: the plate, the default view at phase 0, and a right view (yaw +50°, pitch 20°, phase 0.15).
  - Middle row: a left view (yaw −50°, pitch −15°), a top view (pitch 70°) and a back view (yaw 180°, phase 0.5).
  - Bottom row: a below view (yaw 30°, pitch −55°, phase 0.8), phase 0.33 and phase 0.66.
- **Whole-cycle strips.** `sheets/ID-s.png` shows eight phases (1/16 … 15/16) in the default view.
- **Zooms.** `z/*.png`.
- **Probes.**
  - `probe.mjs` gives world bounding boxes per role and phase.
  - `proj.mjs` gives screen-space motion, used to check rotation direction.
  - `vis.mjs` gives role visibility over the cycle.
  - `roles.mjs` gives roles and materials.
- **Screens.** All five screens were run over 426–507 with `--jobs=8`: `disc.json`, `cf.json`, `white.json`, `edge.json` and `facet.json`. `disc-digest.txt` is a digest of the disconnected-parts screen. The screens were used as leads only.
- **Server.** A vite server on port 46026, with a private `cacheDir`, was started and stopped by this lane.

Before reporting, I read each ledger row and the p96 audit for this range. Documented forced limits are not re-reported unless a concrete new fix exists.

## Findings

### 431 — medium
- The eight spokes lie in the mid-plane (z ±0.13), but the two rims are tori at z ±0.41…0.61, and there is no drum. Each spoke tip ends in open air between the two rims, touching only the float boards.
- From the top and oblique views, the spokes visibly stop short of the rim. Brown draws the spokes running into the rim.
- Captures: `z/431ez.png` (floats hidden), `z/431cz.png`, `sheets/431.png`.
- Fix: either fork each spoke tip into two short arms that run into both rims, or add a third rim ring in the spoke plane at r ≈ 2.26. The first is closer to 430 and 432, where the spokes end on a closed drum.

### 444 — medium
- The fountain crown is a static solid translucent bullet: 0 changed pixels across phases in the top crop of `strip/444-*`.
- Brown draws a column that breaks into a plume falling away on both sides. The rules say water must flow and not be static.
- Captures: `sheets/444.png`, `sheets/444-s.png`.
- Fix: replace `waterFountainGeometry` in `authored-hydraulic-rams.js` (role `continuous-high-level-water-jet-crown`) with 6–8 animated ballistic `WaterStream`s falling back to the air-vessel level, as 464 does, or give the crown a scrolling streak texture.

### 447 — medium
- In the default plan view, the boat, rudder and anchor rope cast a large, offset boat-shaped shadow on the river bed. It reads as a second, ghost boat above the real one, and the bank casts a dark trapezoid across the water.
- Captures: `sheets/447.png` (default and phase tiles).
- Fix: stop the bed and water receiving shadows from the boat group, or make the water opaque enough that the bed shadow does not show. The alternative is a much shallower bed, so the shadow falls under the hull.

### 452 — medium
- The four numbered check flaps (1–4), which are the working parts, are thin translucent slivers tucked into the port corners. At the default size they are nearly invisible.
- Brown draws them as bold slanted flaps. Probes show each flap is about 0.07 thick and turns only a few hundredths.
- Captures: `sheets/452.png`, `z/452fz.png`.
- Fix: make the flaps opaque with a contrasting colour, about 2× thicker, and hinged visibly as in 448–451. Keep the section water behind them, not in front.

### 465 — medium
- The operator's jacket, arms and forearms are the same orange (#de5a3f) as the hand bar, its stanchions and the beam. The hands merge into the bar, and the figure blends into the beam in every view: orange on orange.
- Captures: `sheets/465.png`, `z/465z.png`.
- Fix: recolour the operator's jacket and arms to a contrasting cloth colour, such as dark blue-grey or brown. Alternatively, make the hand bar steel grey.

### 506 — medium
- The driver-shaft bearing is a black round drum with a grey square bridge butting into its side. This is a disc perched on a block, which the rules forbid.
- The coincident-face screen flags `driver-shaft-A-bearing` against `driver-bearing-bridge-to-curved-standard` (area 0.038, contrast 0.23), and moiré is visible at the joint in the zoom.
- Captures: `z/506cz.png`.
- Fix: make the bridge and the bearing boss one casting, with the bar running tangent into a round boss plus a fillet. Alternatively, sink the bar's end inside the boss so no faces coincide.

### 432 — low

- Capture: `sheets/432.png` (back tile).
- Fix: make the back face one polygon, or exclude it from `smoothFacetNormals`.

### 433 — low
