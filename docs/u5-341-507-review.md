# Lane u5-341-507: user-feedback fixes (pass 51)

Direct user feedback on movements rated flawed in the four-phase review
(`/dev/shm/assess3`). Each fix was checked in captures at 4–16 phases per
cycle (default view, render beside the plate). Motion complaints were also
checked by sampling the motion numerically.

## 354: uniform-velocity endless groove
- The depth mirror (`scale: [1, 1, -1]`) is removed. The view is now taken
  from the crosshead side: the grooved crosshead and its stem sit in front of
  an **opaque** disk. The disk's raised rim now faces the viewer and reads as
  Brown's ring; it was translucent and in front before. The whole endless
  groove and the wrist running in it are visible at every phase. The guide
  bridges moved to the front face.
- Framing: `cameraFitBounds`/`sweptBounds` are the 64-sample sweep of the
  disk, crosshead, stems and guides, so both stem ends stay in frame for the
  whole stroke (max NDC 0.87).
- The stems are already the minimum length that stays in the guides, so
  framing the whole stroke makes the figure smaller than Brown's cropped plate.
- Intersections: 0.0000 before and after (shaft touching the plate).
- Residual: the raised strap that retains the groove's island is now visible
  along the stem line.

## 355: gyroscope
- The fit changed from the opening-pose crop to the whole precession sweep. A
  proxy box sized to the circle's projection stops the square box's corners
  from inflating the fit. Max NDC is 0.91 at the widest phase, and disk C and
  ring A stay in view throughout.

## 384: helicograph
