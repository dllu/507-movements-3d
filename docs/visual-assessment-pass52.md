# Pass-52 visible assessment

After the user's 2026-09-24 review, every movement was re-rated from fresh
production captures at four phases of its cycle plus an oblique view, beside
its engraving. Ratings: **reasonable** (looks and works right, at most invisible
modelling limits), **minor** (small visible differences), **flawed** (clear
visible or motion defects: wrong shape, wrong or jerky motion, parts not
engaging or disconnected, excessive speed, poor framing). Physics caveats such
as friction or loads moved to each row's limits. Per-row text lives in
[movement-status.json](movement-status.json).

Observations beyond single rows:

  visibly too fast on 186–189 (≈9×); on 171, 172, 190, 203, 217, 220, 245, 249,
