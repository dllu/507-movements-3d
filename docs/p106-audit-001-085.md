### 078: medium
- **Flaw:** the right (short) pawl never drops into a tooth root: across its whole working stroke its foot rides on the back of a tooth near the tip. The two strokes are therefore very unequal: wheel A advances 9.2° on the left pawl's stroke (phase 0.53–0.75) but only 2.6° on the right pawl's (0.9–0.25), although both pawl pins sit about 0.44 from the rocker pivot. As a result the "double-acting" second pawl does almost nothing.
- **Captures:** `/dev/shm/p106/r001/zD/m078r.png` (right pawl foot at phases 0.78–0.25), `/dev/shm/p106/r001/zD/m078.png` panels c and f, `/dev/shm/p106/r001/zD/strip078s.png`.
- **Fix:** re-time or lengthen the right pawl so that at the start of its stroke (rocker about −20°) its toe drops into the root in front of a tooth's radial face. Its length/rest angle should be set so that each stroke carries the wheel about half a pitch. Then re-run the finite ratchet contact.
