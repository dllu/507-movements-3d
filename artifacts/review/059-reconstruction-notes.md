Production 059 is now integrated in `src/simulation/two-speed-selector.js`,
`two-speed-selector-motion.js` and `two-speed-selector-geometry.js`. The
`059-candidate-*.mjs` files re-export that production code. The superseded shared

and this is an unfinished review of the full catalog.

## Source arrangement

[The official page](https://507movements.com/mm_059.html), saved in
`../reference/mm_059.html`, has no available official animation. The larger
unchanged Brown image is `../reference/brown-059-detail.png`, extracted from
PDF page 22 (one-based), scale 6000, crop (3100,3730,1300,1360).

A broad upper pulley drives a flat band across three equal lower pulleys. The
leftmost is loose. The middle pulley drives the main shaft and the small gear
at the left. The rightmost drives a hollow shaft and the large gear at the
right. Both output gears share the lower shaft. This gives slow and quick
output modes; the unselected input member must back-drive from the output.
The gear pairs are on opposite sides of the lower pulleys, unlike 058.

The source is a side elevation with the small input gear on the left. In the
current world-z shaft convention, it needs a negative-x camera. It shows a
flat band with a schematic torn-end interruption, equal smooth lower treads
without obstructing traverse flanges, and one broad upper drum. The lower
pulleys are much larger relative to the gear spacing than in the old model.
The side-view tooth hatching cannot establish tooth counts.

## Confirmed old-model defects

directions over 129 phases per pair, each covering one relative engagement.

- The 18/38 pair has 52,113 penetrating samples in 3,041,820 checks, maximum
  depth 0.036862706.
- The 38/18 pair has 52,141 penetrating samples in 3,041,820 checks, maximum
  depth 0.036862816.

Total: **104,254 penetrations in 6,083,640 checks**. The same quarter-pitch
mounting error found in old 058 persists in this separate branch. Speed ratios
and phase identities in the old regression test do not establish actual tooth
clearance.

`059-hardware-baseline.json` is a scoped one-direction check: initial shaft
surfaces against other input-member gears, then the actual round-band skin
against all driver treads and lower pulley bodies/rims through 97 selector

- The band penetrates all three driver treads, up to depth 0.040600819.
- During traversal it penetrates four lower torus flanges, up to depth
  0.039152613.
- This initial scoped shaft sampling does not find the shaft/gear intrusion
  seen in 058. It is not a complete bearing or hardware clearance proof.

The baseline band changes its actual position buffer in place. The harness

## Reconstructed assembly

`059-candidate-model.mjs`, `059-candidate-motion.mjs` and
`059-candidate-geometry.mjs` now re-export the production 13-solid reconstruction.
Its 12/42 and 45/9 gear pairs use module 0.049351851851851856, center distance
1.3325, ordinary 30-degree involutes, rounded rack-generated roots and 0.00002
bilateral backlash. The 20-degree nine-tooth profile failed the feasibility
check: its largest working gap was 0.000574437 and normal-force power error
was about 170 percent. The 25-degree alternative kept contact but had a larger
0.811-percent sampled power error than the 30-degree profile's 0.283 percent.
These comparisons are in `059-profile-options.json`; they are not source
claims about tooth counts or pressure angle.

The source projection uses (750,828.5), 200 pixels per unit, looking along
negative x. Gear planes are z=-1.56 and 1.66, with depths 0.425 and 0.40.
Main-shaft and sleeve outer radii are 0.1425 and 0.2725; the sleeve and loose
pulley have real 0.1485-radius bores. The output shaft radius is 0.1375.
Both solid shafts span z=-1.925..1.975. The independent sleeve runs from
z=0.26 to 1.86, including its attached right pulley and large input gear.

Three smooth lower pulleys have radius 1.125, width 0.48, and centers at
z=-0.5,0,0.5. The upper drum has the same radius, lies 2.91875 above the input,
and spans z=-0.80..0.925. Its shaft radius is 0.1325, spanning z=-1.395..1.625.
The continuous flat band is 0.26 wide and 0.018 thick with a 0.00015 radial
clearance. Mesh translation moves it between lanes; vertex colors convey belt
material flow without protruding marker solids. Ground is hidden, source FOV
is 7 degrees, and the alternate oblique direction is (-8,3,6).

Selection order is neutral, slow, quick, slow. Each 2.4-second authored dwell
advances the driver a quarter revolution through quintic easing; each stopped
traverse lasts 0.9 seconds, giving 13.2 authored seconds per demonstration.
The output ratios are -2/7 and -5; in quick mode the main input back-drives at
17.5 times the driver speed. The reduced driver excursion keeps the required
motion readable when final display pacing is measured. This is an inferred
operating sequence, not an official animation. Bearing supports, axial retention,
friction and inertia remain ideal constraints.

## Candidate verification and corrections

The first solid audit found two cap triangles of area 7.93e-24 in the 42-tooth
output gear. Their near-collinearity came from a sin(pi) axis residue of
1.33e-16. The candidate's geometry helper snaps only local coordinate magnitudes
below 1e-14 to zero before Float32 contour cleanup and cap triangulation. The
final `059-candidate-solids.json` passes all 13 parts: positive volume,
nondegenerate faces, outward corner normals and paired oriented edges. The
initial solid report is retained as `059-candidate-solids-initial.json`.
Production 058's geometry helper was not modified.

The corrected `059-candidate-hardware.json` passes 97 full demonstration
poses over 66 independent hardware pairs: **97,609,164 bidirectional surface
samples, zero penetrations**. The matching tooth pairs are checked separately;
rigidly attached same-member solids may share cylindrical joining surfaces.
The pre-cleanup hardware report is retained with an `-initial` suffix.

The final `059-candidate-gear-contact.json` checks 2,049 phases on each of
both flanks of both pairs: **8,196 rows, zero contour crossings**, with actual
Float32 gaps 24.016 to 28.794 microunits and maximum pairwise normal-force power
residual **0.317 percent**. Sampling uses the quick dwell so each pair advances
a complete output-tooth period. An earlier harness copied the slow dwell from
058, but that dwell advances only 9/14 of one tooth of the nine-tooth output.
The insufficient sweep is retained as `059-candidate-gear-contact-short-sweep.json`
and must not be treated as full-cycle evidence. The corrected script explicitly
rejects sample progress outside its selected dwell.

Independent `059-candidate-exact-contact.json` checks four actual 3D gear
contacts, six selected driver/lower-pulley contacts and four mid-traverse
contacts on both neighboring treads. All have positive working gaps: gears
24.591 to 28.057 microunits; band/tread gaps about 145.49 to 145.58 microunits.
These finite sweeps and tessellation checks do not solve inertia, elasticity
or friction, or certify arbitrary unsampled collision freedom.

All five candidate source/oblique/rear/shift/quick-mode captures have been
inspected. They retain both gear pairs, source ordering, large smooth pulleys,
real shaft bores and a clear continuous flat band. The inspected interactive
`059-source-alignment.html` overlays actual mesh side envelopes on the
unchanged Brown scan; all measured top/bottom residuals are within 7.66 pixels.
Its rectangular side envelopes do not depict internal tooth lines or hidden
bores. The companion JSON records the residuals explicitly.
