# Movement 087: weighted self-reversing clutch

The unchanged production model requires reconstruction. Its central bevel
driver has a vertical input shaft. In the engraving, the driver appears as a
face-on ring behind the opposed loose gears B and C, in the same source plane
as wheel E and the weighted lever/bell-crank linkage. The front render shows
the resulting mismatch particularly clearly: an upper edge-on cone replaces
the large background ring. Rotating the whole model does not fix the relative
driver/lever/E orientation.

All three saved production views (default, front and oblique) and Brown PDF
page 30, printed page 26, are inspected. The native page also shows the central
ring extending above and below the side cones and larger than E. Those outline
observations justify measuring new pitch geometry; they do not establish an
exact tooth ratio. No geometry has been changed from this preliminary reading.

The source describes an output stud striking bell crank G, a connecting rod
tipping weighted lever F over center, and gravity shifting feathered clutch D
between loose gears B and C. The subsequent mechanical review must cover
finite bevel contact, jaw engagement and release, the stud/lever interaction,
and independent hardware clearances over both reversal directions. Existing
tests assert several current implementation choices, including the vertical
input axis; they are not independent evidence of source fidelity.

`087-inspected-baseline.json` records the three inspected images and exact
source hashes. All 972 frozen production inputs remain unchanged. This is a
static baseline, with no new motion, clearance, performance, build or full-suite
acceptance. The wider 507-movement review remains active.
