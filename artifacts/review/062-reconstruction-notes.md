# 062 · Enclosed dual-input differential reconstruction

Production 062 is rebuilt and verified. All 2,984 numerical tests and all 22
browser tests pass, including the full 507-canvas sweep. The twelve integrated
comparison frames, both source overlays and desktop/mobile controls have been
visually inspected. The final record is generated by
`scripts/record-dual-input-differential-review.mjs` from actual evidence files.
The catalog remains unfinished; 037 is unresolved and 063 onward are pending.

The original 16,790-character wrapper factory and
14,302-character existing test are archived as `062-original-factory.txt`
and `062-original-test.txt`. Parameters, display timing and camera are in
`062-baseline-parameters.json`. The original also used the legacy differential
base archived in `061-original-factory.txt`. Both obsolete factories were removed
after exact comparison with the archives, accounting for the base's temporary
name `legacyDifferentialSelectorForMovement62`. Rebuilt 061 and 062 use separate
constructors. The baseline and provisional-study sections below are historical.

## Source

[The official page](https://507movements.com/mm_062.html), saved as
`../reference/mm_062.html`, explicitly marks its animation unavailable.
Brown's unchanged enlargement is `../reference/brown-062-detail.png`, from
PDF page 24 (one-based), scale 6000, crop (3140,980,1200,1460). It has been
visually inspected. The printed description is on PDF page 25 (printed 21),
saved as `../reference/brown-page-25.txt`.

This extends 061 by connecting its formerly held side bevel to a fourth
lower pulley. A smaller upper pulley drives that fourth pulley with a
second band. For equal opposed side gears, output plus side-input speed
equals twice carrier speed. An open auxiliary band subtracts the side
input from twice carrier speed; crossing it reverses the side input and
adds its magnitude. The main direct pulley provides the other working speed.

The engraving shows a broad upper drum, a smaller upper drum extending well
to its right, four adjacent large lower pulleys, two flat bands and compact
dashed bevel structure inside the lower bank. There is no external brake
weight or support frame. Torn band ends are drafting interruptions. Exact
hidden bearing details and tooth counts are unspecified. The original
positive-x camera reverses the source's axial order.

## Baseline contact findings

`062-hardware-baseline.json` samples 97 full demonstration poses. It checks
the main band against all upper treads/lower bodies and rims, the installed
auxiliary band against its pulleys, and selected independent shaft, sleeve
and arm pairs. Dynamic position-version changes refresh cached surfaces.
There are **543,767 penetrating samples in 9,924,238 checks**. This is a
scoped defect probe, not an all-pair clearance proof.

- The main band penetrates upper treads by up to 0.040637401 and lower
  selector torus flanges by up to 0.038034003 during shifts.
- The auxiliary band penetrates its upper pulley body by up to 0.038670154.
- The carrier sleeve passes through the output bevel body and hub:
  99,328 penetrating samples each, maximum depths 0.231682313 and 0.091444498.
- The auxiliary-input sleeve occupies part of the carrier sleeve's solid
  annulus: 114,952 penetrating samples in that direction, depth 0.026991868.
  Their nominally coincident cylindrical skins produce shallower reverse
  penetration too. These are independently rotating parts.
- The carrier arm crosses the output shaft (485 penetrating samples).

`062-exact-hardware-baseline.json` checks actual triangle skins, including
edge/edge and segment/face intersection. Four selected pairs have zero
distance: planet axle/output shaft, carrier arm/output shaft, carrier
sleeve/side-input sleeve, and carrier sleeve/output-gear body. The separate
side-input-gear-body/output-shaft query finds a lower bound of 0.01 without
a witness; it does not show an intersection for that pair.

`062-belt-contact-baseline.json` checks both installed bands against their
selected upper and lower bodies at six dwell midpoints, covering all three
selections in both auxiliary-band orientations. All twelve upper-body
queries have zero distance. Every lower-body query has a substantial gap:
main band about 0.020331, auxiliary band 0.021271–0.021380. Thus the round
bands intersect the upper drums while floating above the lower body treads.
The old lower pulley body is radius 0.549; the nominal band path is radius
0.61, with only the remote side torus flanges reaching that nominal radius.

The old crossed round band already uses opposite axial lifts on its free
spans. A separate actual-triangle query on disjoint lifted central span
skins finds a positive gap of 0.066317. It does not establish entire-loop
or marker clearance and should not be described as a self-intersection
defect. A replacement flat band's width and orientation will need their own
crossing and pulley-contact checks.

`062-bevel-contact-baseline.json` covers one complete relative planet-tooth
pitch in each auxiliary orientation, with 65 phases per orientation. The
harness inverts the actual direct-stage delta separately for open and
crossed cases. Both mesh pairs are checked in both directions: **3,515,200
surface checks, zero penetrating samples**, per-pair minimum gaps
0.001299938–0.001315194. The old zero-radius bore contributes 768 collapsed
faces, excluded from nearest-surface evaluation. This confirms sampled tooth
clearance, not tight working contact or manufactured conjugacy.

## Requirements identified during baseline review

Use smooth source-sized drums and permanent flat bands, real nested bores,
and a radial spindle that stops outside the output shaft. The gear train
must fit inside hollow lower pulleys with a useful section view. Preserve
the source's subtract/add law using actual belt pitch radii and complementary
bevel pitch cones. Check both loaded flanks, actual normal-force power,
all independent hardware and working band contact.

Neutral requires an explicit idealization: an auxiliary-driven differential
with both output and carrier free has one unconstrained motion. The old model
holds output speed zero and back-drives the carrier at half the side-input
speed. That is a selected load/initial-condition assumption, not a unique
consequence of the differential gearing. Do not copy it as a derived law.

The old animation hides the auxiliary band during two stopped re-reeving
intervals, then changes between open and crossed routes. A replacement
should present those as separate installed configurations or explicitly
model a feasible re-reeving operation; a continuously driven belt cannot
change topology through a disappearance. All three actual baseline captures at phases 0, 0.29 and 0.78 are saved
and visually inspected (062-baseline-phase-*.png). They were captured only
after 061's browser wrapper exited zero. They show the reversed axial order,
undersized lower pulleys, narrow spoked auxiliary driver, round bands and
oversized exposed bevel train outside the source enclosure. The crossed
configuration at phase 0.78 is included.

## Initial isolated dimension study

proposal in `062-provisional-layout.json`. At 200 pixels/unit it uses
concentric upper/lower axes at source y=408 and 1066, source-sized radii
1.385 (main upper), 0.725 (auxiliary upper), and 1.425 (lower), plus real
0.006 radial bearing clearances. These measured drum/shaft outline residuals
are at most 13.5 scan pixels. They exclude band arches and inferred internals.

The main selector band need not be centered on every lower pulley. Proposed
selected z positions -0.55,-0.04,0.435 keep its full 0.30 width on the broad
upper drum and each lower tread, including the upper drum's short right edge.
The carrier selection retains 0.015 upper-edge and 0.005 lower-edge margins.
Actual neutral-fiber radii give main ratio 0.972128349 and auxiliary magnitude
0.512246107, hence carrier-selected output ratios 1.432010591 with an open
auxiliary band and 2.456502805 with a crossed one.

A provisional 34/20-tooth complementary bevel pair has side axial extent
0.215..0.35 and a common apex at z=0.45. The actual generated root of the
side gear clears the proposed carrier web by 0.013867405. The radial planet
spindle starts 0.0325 outside the main shaft. These are necessary dimension
checks, not assembled collision, conjugacy or force evidence; no replacement
factory or control is integrated.

Separate open/crossed installed configurations will need an accessible
control, a deterministic restart, camera bounds containing both routes and
display-speed measurements covering the faster crossed configuration. The
current generic controls support section switching but no drive configuration
selector. No user-interface or timing-measurement changes have been made.

## Integrated construction

`src/simulation/dual-input-differential.js` now builds the enclosed transmission;
`dual-input-differential-motion.js` supplies its pure motion. The candidate entry
points re-export these production files. Two permanent flat bands connect the
source-sized drums. Real bored sleeves separate output, carrier and side input.
The radial planet spindle starts outside the main shaft and terminates in the
carrier rim. Real bore and axial clearances replace the overlapping legacy solids.

The integrated dimensions retain the study's measured radii and ratios. A 34-tooth
side bevel pair and 20-tooth planet fit inside the two large hollow lower cases.
Their complementary pitch cones use 20-degree back-cone involute profiles,
64 flank subdivisions and 16 tip subdivisions, with genuinely conical heel/toe
ends. Smooth turned bodies use 512 angular divisions. These are inferred hidden
dimensions and approximate bevel profiles, not a claim that Brown specified a
particular manufactured tooth system.

The main band occupies z=-0.55,-0.04,0.435 for loose, direct and carrier selections.
Both bands have width 0.30 and thickness 0.02. The auxiliary band occupies the
fourth lower pulley and is always visible. Its crossed free spans use opposite
axial bows of 0.24; their full physical triangles are included in the clearance
checks. Band routing and twist are prescribed geometry, without elastic or
friction equilibrium calculations.

Section view clips only the two hollow lower pulley cases and adds fixed,
nonphysical cut caps. The full physical geometries remain closed, and toggling
section view does not change their transforms. Complete view shows the enclosure.
The camera follows the engraving's negative-x side, and the ground is hidden.

## Motion, configuration control and playback

For both installed auxiliary-band configurations, actual neutral-fiber radii give
main ratio 0.972128348953 and side-input magnitude 0.512246106679. The equal side
gears enforce output + side input = 2 carrier. Consequently carrier selection
gives output/input ratios 1.432010591227 open and 2.456502804585 crossed. Direct
selection retains the main-band ratio while the other differential members move
as required by the same equation.

The demonstrator explicitly stops the input in neutral and throughout every
selector shift. This avoids assigning a purported unique speed to a free
auxiliary-driven differential. Stopping is an operator/demo assumption. All
shafts and band circulation are stationary in neutral; only the selector's axial
position changes during a shift. Quintic easing makes position and speed
continuous, and seeking is a pure function of time, including negative times.

The new accessible **Auxiliary belt** select offers **Open** and **Crossed**.
Changing it restarts time while preserving playback and section states. It
replaces and disposes the old auxiliary-band geometry on the same visible mesh;
there is no animated disappearance or implicit re-reeving operation. The engine
refreshes camera bounds and floor clearance after the change. Desktop and mobile
layouts, pause/play, orbiting and section controls are exercised by the browser
test.

The complete authored demonstration lasts 11.3 time units. Display measurement
now evaluates every offered configuration, uses the maximum sustained speed
across configurations and unions their bounds. The resulting scale is
2.366738630771 and the displayed cycle is **4.774502707263 seconds**. The faster
crossed installation limits the scale; its measured peak is about 2.499 turns
per displayed second. Actual shaft speeds in both configurations remain below
three turns/second in the focused playback test. Other models retain their
existing single-configuration measurement path.
