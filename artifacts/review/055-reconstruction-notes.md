# 055 reconstruction evidence

Movement 055 is rebuilt and registered from src/simulation/coaxial-gears.js.
All 2,945 numerical tests pass with explicit exit code 0 (117.014 seconds),
the build passes (8.74 seconds), and all 15 browser tests pass (10.8 minutes),
including all 507 canvases and the dedicated 055 playback/orbit/mobile test.
The final source, oblique, rear and interactive source-overlay views are
inspected. The gallery contains 214 comparisons. 037 remains unresolved;
056 onward still require review. The overall goal is active.

Source and interpretation

Brown describes two coaxial gears A and C driven at different speeds by B:
https://507movements.com/mm_055.html. The website corrects Brown's D/B label
typo. Its animation uses 20/10/40 teeth; neither description fixes a ratio.
The enlarged Brown engraving's boundary pitch strongly supports A17 and
C37. B is partly occluded, but its boundary favors 10; common-module
geometry independently requires B=(C-A)/2=10. The 17/10/37 reconstruction
follows the engraving rather than the website animation's counts.
See 055-source-pitch-measurement.json and ../reference/brown-055-detail.png.

The geometry uses module 0.1, fixed center distance 1.35, and C outer radius
2.075. Shaft radii are A 0.26 and B 0.13. The frame is rotated by
atan2(16.5,227.5), matching B's slightly lower source position. The source
alignment overlay fixes scale to Brown's outer rim and shows actual
rendered contours. It also exposes remaining differences in tooth shape
and phase: the engraving is schematic, while the model uses mechanically
compatible involutes. No claim of exact stroke-by-stroke coincidence is made.

Working tooth geometry

All gears use a common 25-degree pressure angle. A and B are generated by
a rack with tangent circular tips; C has analytic internal involutes,
addendum 0.08 and dedendum 0.105. The rounded cutter retains the working
flanks that a full-depth sharp tool overcuts on a low-count pinion. Its tip
radius is limited to 95% of the radius where its bottom fillets meet.
The 0.38-module option at 20 degrees follows KHK's basic-rack fillet; the
25-degree pressure angle and shortened internal tips are reconstruction
choices, not dimensions supplied by Brown:
https://khkgears.net/new/gear_knowledge/gear_technical_reference/involute_gear_profile.html
The source is saved in ../reference/khk-involute-gear-profile.html.

C has 64 samples per flank and 8 per tip/root arc. The shared internal-gear
helper accepts these optional counts and omits degenerate bevel layers
when chamfer is zero. Existing callers retain their sampling and bevels.
The existing jaw-clutch spur generator and its defaults are unchanged.

Loaded phases add +0.000928 radians to A and -0.000417 to C. These are
constant offsets, with no timing correction. B turns at pi rad/s, A at
-pi*10/17, and C at pi*10/37: one input revolution every two seconds,
with A and C taking 3.4 and 7.4 seconds respectively.

Support construction

The hidden cup web and sleeve are explicitly inferred. A/B gear slabs are
Z=-0.09..0.09; C's rim is -0.13..0.09; the web is -0.23..-0.13; the sleeve
is -0.56..-0.18. A's shaft extends -0.65..0.125, B's -0.09..0.125. B thus
ends ahead of the rotating web. The web front is pale paint on existing
faces, with no extra geometry. External bearings are idealized beyond the
shown shaft ends. Each gear and its own shaft form one rigid assembly.
A's shaft clears the independent 0.263 web bore and 0.267 sleeve bore.

Independent evidence

- 055-actual-contour-contact.json: 2,049 phases per pair; contours recovered
  from actual Float32 side-wall triangles. A gap 0.00002455..0.00003151,
  C gap 0.00002774..0.00003812; no crossings. Compressive forces give
  positive input/output power; maximum relative power residual is 0.2881%.
- 055-planar-method-validation.json: exact segment minima agree with
  independent triangle BVH minima at 34 pair/pose combinations to 5.9e-17.
  055-sharp-triangle-contour-validation.json repeats the comparison using
  contours recovered directly from triangles. Six independent 3D minima
  on the final candidate are in 055-final-candidate-triangle-contact.json.
- 055-candidate-hardware.json: 97 poses across a full C revolution,
  14 independently moving hardware pairs, 38,887,646 bidirectional checks,
  zero penetration. Tooth engagements are checked separately.
- 055-candidate-solids.json: all seven solids have closed oriented edges,
  positive volume, nonzero triangles and outward normals at every corner.
- 055-mechanical-tests.log: four focused tests pass. Full test/build/browser
  results and capture links are collected in 055-reconstruction.json.

These are finite numerical checks with small tessellation clearances.
The force calculation is quasistatic, not a simulation of inertia,
elasticity, wear or impact. It does not certify the rest of the catalogue.

Preserved failures

The old rectangular internal teeth and quarter-pitch phase errors caused
43,522 external and 9,813 internal penetrating samples in 6,591,450 checks.
The rear spokes also collided with B's hub: 184 penetrating samples in
78,432 checks. The original factory, tests, captures and reports are saved.
The first sharp-rack candidate had a varying external gap up to 0.001615;
a rounded 20-degree candidate still lost engagement and interfered at C's
tips. They were rejected before integration. Detailed intermediate notes
remain in 055-reconstruction-history.md.
