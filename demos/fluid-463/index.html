About this experiment +

Real 3D particles respond to gravity, neighboring water, and solid gate and channel surfaces. The visible surface is reconstructed from those particles. Automatic gates use an approximate hydrostatic torque model; this prototype is not a validated reconstruction or engineering fluid model. Inlet and outlet account for particles added and removed.

Runs in WebGL using the existing Three.js dependency. Implementation and limitations ↗
